# Asking ADR (MyNeta) for permission to use their affidavit data

ADR's terms of use (https://adrindia.org/content/adr-terms-use) allow free non-commercial use with citation, but
forbid commercial reuse and any systematic or automated collection (scraping) without ADR's express written consent.
Southwala shows ads, so we need that consent before we use their data in bulk.

Send from your own email (the site owner). Suggested text:

---
Subject: Request for written permission: using MyNeta election affidavit data on Southwala.com

Dear ADR team,

I run Southwala (southwala.com), an independent news and explainer site for Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka,
Tamil Nadu and Kerala. We want to publish a public profile page for every sitting MLA and MP of Andhra Pradesh and
Telangana, showing the declared assets, liabilities, education and declared cases from their election affidavits,
and a party-wise comparison, with the same template for every party.

We would like your express written permission to:
1. Use the winners' summary data published on MyNeta (name, constituency, party, declared cases, education,
   total assets, liabilities) for Andhra Pradesh 2024, Telangana 2023 and the Lok Sabha 2024 members from both states.
2. Collect it in an automated way, once per election cycle (a few page requests, at a slow rate).
3. Publish it on a site that carries advertising.

In return we will: credit "Association for Democratic Reforms (MyNeta.info)" with a link to the original
candidate page on every profile; not use ADR's logo or imply endorsement; state clearly that criminal cases listed
are cases declared in affidavits and are allegations unless a conviction is stated; carry a right of reply; and send
you the bibliographic details of our publication as your terms ask.

Please let us know whether this is possible, and on what conditions.

Thank you,
[Your name], Southwala
[Your phone / email]
---
