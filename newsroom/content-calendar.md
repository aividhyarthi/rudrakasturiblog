# Southwala content calendar (draft for planning)

## How many a day (as scheduled, checked 7 Nov 2026)
Counted from the `date` in each article file, all times IST:
- **1 to 12 Nov: 7 to 10 stories a day** (a busy run for Deepavali and Karthika). Usual times: 07:00, 08:00, 09:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00, 19:00, with 10:00, 14:00 and 21:00 added on festival days.
- **13 Nov to 11 Dec: 4 stories a day**, at 07:00, 11:00, 15:00 and 19:00. The last scheduled stories are on 11 Dec.
- **Plus automatic pages every day:** horoscope today and tomorrow, daily panchangam, tithi and festival pages.

The hourly rebuild publishes each story at its time; the home page rotates four times a day.

## The vow series (17:00 slot)
Real vows, named honestly as tradition, no promised results:
Santoshi Mata, 16 Fridays (no sour food); Vaibhav Lakshmi, 11 or 21 Fridays; Sai Baba, 9 Thursdays; Solah Somvar, 16 Mondays; Shani, 7 Saturdays; Hanuman, Tuesdays; Pradosham, 11 or 24 months; Sankashti. For each: where it comes from, the rules as families keep them, the story, what if a day is missed, and a plain line that faith is the point, not a guarantee.

## Unique topics (the deeper reads)
Method for each: name the source (parva, kanda, chapter), compare the tellings (Valmiki, Kamba, Tulsi, Telugu and Kannada versions), say what is not known, then bring it to the reader's life. A second pass checks every fact.
Mahabharata: Vidura Niti, Kanika Niti, the Ashtavakra Gita itself, who stayed out of the war and why (Balarama, Rukmi, Vidura, Barbarika in folk tellings), the Yaksha Prashna, Shakuni, Dhritarashtra's love for his son, Gandhari's blindfold, Draupadi's questions, Ashvatthama.
Ramayana: Ravana and Shiva (the Tandava Stotram, the Atmalinga at Gokarna, Kailasa), Ravana as a scholar, Indrajit and Sulochana, Urmila, Kaikeyi and Manthara, Surpanakha, Maricha, Bharata's paduka rule.
South: Annamayya, Kannappa Nayanar, Karaikal Ammaiyar, Tirumular, the Siddhars, Purandara Dasa, Tyagaraja, Potana, Ramadasu.
Texts: Avadhuta Gita, Yoga Vasistha stories, Vivekachudamani, Bhaja Govindam, Lalita Sahasranama.
