# State of play (verify before relying; reports as of 2026-10-20)
These are office-holders and recent events reported in October 2026. Use web search to confirm anything you rely on. If this list disagrees with a source you can read, trust the source and note the discrepancy in reviewNote.
- Andhra Pradesh: Chandrababu Naidu (TDP-led alliance with Jana Sena and BJP) is chief minister. Local body polls: urban 28 Oct, mandal/zilla parishad 3 Nov, panchayats from 26 Nov.
- Telangana: Revanth Reddy (Congress) is chief minister; the government passed 1,000 days in early September 2026. BRS and BJP are the main opposition.
- Tamil Nadu: Vijay (TVK) has been chief minister since 10 May 2026, with Congress and smaller parties' support. DMK and AIADMK are in opposition.
- Kerala: V.D. Satheesan (Congress, UDF) has been chief minister since May 2026 after UDF won 102 seats. The LDF is in opposition.
- Karnataka: Congress government. One report says D.K. Shivakumar became chief minister in June 2026; this is not confirmed, so verify before naming the chief minister.
