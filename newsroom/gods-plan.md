# Our gods, one by one: writing plan

Series id: `our-gods`. Plain, searchable titles. 900 to 1100 words each. Connection voice (scene opener, "you", questions, admit what is not known, invite readers to write in).
First batch (36) is below. Next batches: Shani, Rahu, Ketu; Narasimha; Dattatreya; Sai Baba; Raghavendra; Annapurna; Gayatri; Hayagriva; Dhanvantari; Kubera; Bhairava; Venkateswara (more); Vitthala (more); Krishna's names; Rama's Ayodhya; each god's stotras and temples.

1. Ganesha: Atharvashirsha in plain words; puja at home; elephant head stories; Sankashti vratam steps
2. Shiva: lingam; Nataraja; Panchakshari; 12 Jyotirlingas; Maha Shivaratri vigil; Dakshinamurthy
3. Devi: Navadurga; Durga Saptashati
4. Vishnu: Dashavatara; Sahasranama; Satyanarayana vratam
5. Venkateswara: Suprabhatam; Vitthala: Pandharpur and Ashadhi wari
6. Krishna: Janmashtami at home; Radha. Rama: Ram Raksha; Maryada Purushottam. Hanuman: stories; two Jayanti dates
7. Murugan: Kanda Shasti Kavasam; Thaipusam and kavadi. Ayyappa: Harihara putra. Lakshmi: Ashtalakshmi; Mahalakshmi Ashtakam. Saraswati: Vidyarambham
8. Navagrahas: Surya, Chandra, Mangala, Budha, Guru, Shukra
