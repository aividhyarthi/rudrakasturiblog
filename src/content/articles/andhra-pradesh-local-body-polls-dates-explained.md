Andhra Pradesh's State Election Commission announced the local body election schedule on 14 October 2026. Here it is in plain words.

## The calendar
- **Urban local bodies (municipalities and corporations):** polling on **28 October**, counting and results on **30 October**. State Election Commissioner Anil Chandra Punetha said notifications for about 100 urban local bodies were issued on 14 October.
- **Mandal and zilla parishads (the block and district councils):** polling on **3 November**, results on **5 November**.
- **Gram panchayats (village councils):** three phases. Most reports give **26 November, 29 November and 2 December**. UNI lists 3 December for the third phase, so check the official notice before you plan around it.

## The rules
The Model Code of Conduct, the rulebook that limits what governments and parties can announce or do during elections, came into force on 14 October, Punetha said. ETV Bharat reported in August that the polls would use the 2025 voters' list.

## Why it matters
These are the votes closest to daily life: town drainage, village water supply, local roads and who controls the local councils. They are also the first real test at the ground level for the parties across the state.

## What to watch next
Nomination and scrutiny schedules for each phase, which we did not find in the reports we reviewed. The State Election Commission's official notification will have them.
