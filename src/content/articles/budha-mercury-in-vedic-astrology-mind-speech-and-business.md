You have a good idea in the morning, and by afternoon you cannot put it into words. Or the opposite: you speak without thinking, and regret it. A friend says, *Mercury must be acting up*, and you laugh.

There is an old idea behind that joke, and it is worth understanding properly.

## The planet
In Vedic astrology, Budha is the planet Mercury. The name also means *the wise one*. Budha is considered the planet of the mind: how we think, speak, learn, write, count and trade. It is the quick planet, which takes only about a year to go around the Zodiac, always close to the Sun.

## What it is said to rule
- **Rashis.** Budha rules Mithuna (Gemini) and Kanya (Virgo).
- **Exaltation.** It is at its strongest in Kanya, and at its weakest in Meena (Pisces).
- **Day.** Wednesday, *Budhavaram*.
- **Colour.** Green is the traditional colour.
- **Nakshatras.** It is linked with Ashlesha, Jyeshtha and Revati.
- **Themes.** Speech, intelligence, education, accounts, communication, humour, trade, young people.

## How to read this without fear
Astrology can make people anxious. Read these traditional associations the way you would read a character sketch: not as a sentence, but as a set of tendencies. A strong Budha in a chart is said to give a sharp mind and a way with words. A troubled Budha is said to bring scattered thoughts or hasty speech. None of this is a prediction that anything will go wrong.

The honest position is that no one can promise how a planet will affect a life. What astrology gives is a language for thinking about our own patterns.

## What people do on Wednesdays
- **Worship Vishnu,** the god traditionally linked with Budha. Many read a few names from the Vishnu Sahasranama.
- **Wear or use green,** or offer green moong to the needy.
- **Learn something.** The simplest way to honour Budha is to read, write or study for half an hour.
- **Watch your speech.** Pause before you reply to the difficult message.

These are customs. They cost nothing, and they do no harm.

## The story of Budha
In the Puranas, Budha is the son of Soma, the Moon, and Tara, the wife of Brihaspati, the teacher of the gods. His birth is the cause of a great quarrel, which is settled when Brahma declares that the child belongs to Soma. Budha later marries Ila, and their son Pururavas is the ancestor of the lunar dynasty. Notice that the planet of intellect is born of a moral tangle. The tradition does not hide that clever people can also get into trouble.

## Budha in a chart: a few ideas
- **Budhaditya.** When the Sun and Mercury sit in the same sign, which happens often because Mercury never goes far from the Sun, astrologers speak of a *Budhaditya yoga*, said to give intelligence and a good name. It is so common that most teachers do not treat it as special by itself.
- **Retrograde (vakri).** When Mercury appears to move backward, which happens a few times a year, popular astrology warns of miscommunication. Careful teachers say to double-check your work, not to fear.
- **Budha's mantra.** *Om Bram Brim Braum Sah Budhaya Namah* is the common one. Many chant it 108 times on a Wednesday.

## A Wednesday habit
If you like the idea of honouring Budha, give half an hour on Wednesdays to something that sharpens the mind: a chapter of a book, a letter you have been meaning to write, a clean-up of your accounts. That is a fine remedy and does no harm.

## Mercury and the modern world
Because Budha is about speech, writing and trade, it is the planet that astrologers link with today's world of messages, phones, accounting and commerce. This is a modern reading, but a natural one. If you work with words or numbers, you might feel at home with this planet. If you are a student, the old advice is simple: study with attention in the morning, and keep your notes tidy. No chart is needed for that.

## A caution
Do not buy a stone or a ring in Mercury's name on someone's fear-filled advice. The idea that a particular gemstone, such as an emerald, can repair a planet is a modern commercial claim, not a classical requirement. If you wear one because you like it, that is your choice. If you wear it because you were frightened, ask again.
