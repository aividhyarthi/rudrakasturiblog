Someone says, *come on Guruvaram*, and a young person from another part of India asks, quietly, *which day is that?* It happens in many families. Here is a simple page to keep.

## The seven days at a glance

| Day | Telugu | Tamil | Kannada | Malayalam | Hindi | Planet |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sunday | Adivaram | Njayiru | Ravivara (Bhanuvara) | Njayar | Ravivar | Sun (Surya) |
| Monday | Somavaram | Thingal | Somavara | Thinkal | Somvar | Moon (Chandra) |
| Tuesday | Mangalavaram | Sevvai | Mangalavara | Chovva | Mangalvar | Mars (Mangala) |
| Wednesday | Budhavaram | Pudhan | Budhavara | Budhan | Budhvar | Mercury (Budha) |
| Thursday | Guruvaram | Viyazhan | Guruvara | Vyazham | Guruvar | Jupiter (Guru) |
| Friday | Shukravaram | Velli | Shukravara | Velli | Shukravar | Venus (Shukra) |
| Saturday | Shanivaram | Sani | Shanivara | Shani | Shanivar | Saturn (Shani) |

In Sanskrit all of them end in *vara* or *vasara*, "day". *Vaaram* in Telugu is the same word. In Tamil the names are the old Tamil names of the planets, which is why Monday is *Thingal*, "the Moon", and Friday *Velli*, "the bright one".

## Why these seven?
The tradition counts seven bodies that move against the sky: the Sun, the Moon, and five planets that can be seen without a telescope. Each rules a day. The order, which you have used all your life without thinking, comes from an ancient way of counting hours that passes through the planets in turn. Many cultures around the world arrived at a similar week. In India each day also gathered its own gods and customs.

## What families do on each day
- **Sunday, Adivaram.** Surya. Water offered to the rising Sun, and the Aditya Hridayam. See [Sunday Surya puja](/aditya-hridayam-sunday-surya-worship-and-meaning/).
- **Monday, Somavaram.** Shiva, with Om Namah Shivaya, abhishekam and bilva leaves. A light fast is common. See [Monday is Shiva's day](/monday-shiva-worship-panchakshari-and-pournami/).
- **Tuesday, Mangalavaram.** Hanuman and Subramanya, courage and a steady mind. See [Hanuman puja on Tuesday](/hanuman-chalisa-meaning-first-five-verses/).
- **Wednesday, Budhavaram.** Ganesha and Vishnu, and Budha for learning. See [Ganesha puja on Wednesday](/why-ganesha-is-worshipped-first/).
- **Thursday, Guruvaram.** The guru, with Dakshinamurthy, Dattatreya, Sai Baba and Raghavendra Swami. See [Guruvaram puja](/thursday-is-for-the-guru-how-to-worship-on-guruvaram/).
- **Friday, Shukravaram.** Lakshmi and Durga, with a lamp at dusk. See [Friday Lakshmi puja](/friday-lakshmi-worship-mantras-and-how-to-do-it/).
- **Saturday, Shanivaram.** Lord Venkateswara, Shani and Hanuman. See [Shanivaram puja](/saturday-venkateswara-shani-hanuman-how-to-worship/).

## A few questions people ask
**Is Guruvaram the same as Thursday?** Yes. In Telugu homes it is also sometimes called *Lakshmivaram*, because a Thursday vratam to Lakshmi is kept in the Margashira month, and *Brihaspativaram* in Sanskrit.

**Why do people avoid certain things on certain days?** Customs vary a great deal. Some families avoid cutting hair or nails on Tuesdays and Saturdays, or washing hair on certain days. These are family and regional habits and not rules of the scriptures, and you will find that two neighbouring families keep different ones. If a custom helps you keep a day with attention, fine. If it only causes worry, you may let it go.

**Does the day of birth matter?** Many Telugu families give a child a first name based on the nakshatra, and a pet name on the day. In astrology the weekday of birth is said to colour a person's temperament, but different traditions say different things.

**What about the day on which a festival falls?** Tithi and nakshatra decide most festivals. The weekday adds a flavour: a Pradosham on a Monday is *Soma Pradosham*, on a Saturday *Shani Pradosham*. See [Pradosham dates](/pradosham/).

## A small habit
The easiest way to make this personal is to give each day a word. Sunday: thanks. Monday: stillness. Tuesday: courage. Wednesday: learning. Thursday: gratitude to a teacher. Friday: care of the home. Saturday: patience. Many people find that a week with a quiet theme for each day is a gentler week.

## Days that carry a festival
Some weekdays are named in festivals. *Karthika Somavaram* is each Monday in the month of Karthika. *Varalakshmi Vratam* falls on a Friday in Shravana. *Shani Amavasya* and *Somavati Amavasya* are new moon days that fall on a Saturday or a Monday ([Amavasya dates](/amavasya/)). *Mangala Gauri vratam* is kept on Tuesdays in Shravana by newly married women in many homes. When the weekday and the tithi meet, the day is felt to be stronger, and the household makes more of it.

## How to remember the order
A simple trick: the days run Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn. In Telugu, Tamil and Kannada you can simply think *Sun, Moon, Fire, Mind, Teacher, Beauty, Time*. Mars is the planet of fire and energy, Mercury of mind, Jupiter of the teacher, Venus of beauty and Saturn of time. Children learn them faster with these words.
