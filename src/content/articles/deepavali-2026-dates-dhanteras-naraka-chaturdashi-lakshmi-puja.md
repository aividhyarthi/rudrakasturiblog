Deepavali, the festival of lights, is celebrated over several days. The dates below are worked out from the tithis in Hyderabad. Calendars differ by a day in some places, so confirm with your local panchangam or temple.

## The days
| Day | Date | Tithi |
|---|---|---|
| Dhanteras (Dhana Trayodashi) | Friday, 6 November | Trayodashi, 10:30 AM on 6 Nov to 10:48 AM on 7 Nov |
| Naraka Chaturdashi | Sunday, 8 November (before sunrise) | Chaturdashi until 11:28 AM on 8 Nov |
| Deepavali Amavasya, Lakshmi Puja | Sunday, 8 November (evening) | Amavasya, 11:28 AM on 8 Nov to 12:31 PM on 9 Nov |
| Bali Padyami, Govardhan Puja | Tuesday, 10 November | Shukla Pratipada |
| Bhai Dooj (Yama Dwitiya) | Wednesday, 11 November | Shukla Dwitiya |

In Hyderabad, sunset on 8 November is at 5:41 PM. Some calendars place Naraka Chaturdashi on 7 November, so check your local panchangam.

## What each day is for
- **Dhanteras:** the day to honour Lakshmi and Dhanvantari, and to buy something new, traditionally a metal item. Light a lamp at the entrance in the evening.
- **Naraka Chaturdashi:** an early oil bath before sunrise, remembering Krishna's victory over the demon Narakasura, followed by new clothes and lamps.
- **Lakshmi Puja:** on the evening of Amavasya, after sunset and within the pradosha period of about two hours, the home is lit with lamps and Lakshmi and Ganesha are worshipped. Your priest or local panchangam will name the exact auspicious muhurtham.
- **Bali Padyami:** the day after, remembering King Bali, and the first day of Karthika Masam in the Telugu calendar.
- **Bhai Dooj:** sisters pray for their brothers, who visit them.

## Preparing for Lakshmi Puja
1. Clean and decorate the home, with rangoli at the entrance.
2. Place a kalasha, a picture of Lakshmi and Ganesha, and fresh flowers.
3. Light lamps at dusk, including at the doorway.
4. Chant *Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah* and the verses on our [Lakshmi mantras page](/mantras/lakshmi/).
5. Share sweets and prasad.

## Deepavali Amavasya
Because Lakshmi Puja is on Amavasya, this is also the day when families remember ancestors. See the [Amavasya timings for November 2026](/amavasya/november-2026/). The next day begins [Karthika Masam](/karthika-masam-2026-dates-deepam-and-fasting/).

*Traditions vary by region and family.*
