Dhruva was still a young boy when he experienced rejection inside his own family. What began as hurt and a desire to prove himself became a journey of intense devotion to Lord Vishnu.

His story is one of the Bhagavatam's best-known accounts of a child's determination turning towards God.

## A child's hurt

Dhruva was the son of King Uttanapada. One day, he wanted to sit on his father's lap. His stepmother Suruchi stopped him and told him that he could not claim that place unless he was born from her.

The words deeply hurt Dhruva. He went to his mother, Suniti, who guided him towards devotion rather than hatred. She told him to seek the Lord's grace.

## Narada guides Dhruva

Dhruva went into the forest. The sage Narada met him and guided him in spiritual practice. Dhruva fixed his mind on Lord Vishnu and performed intense tapasya.

His devotion became so focused that the story describes the whole universe being affected by the power of his austerity.

## The Lord appears

Pleased with Dhruva's devotion, Lord Vishnu appeared before him. Dhruva had initially wanted a position greater than that of his father and grandfather. But when he received the Lord's darshan, his desire changed.

He realised that the presence of the Lord was far greater than the reward he had originally sought.

## What the story teaches

Dhruva's story begins with a very human emotion: the pain of being rejected. The spiritual lesson is not that children should respond to hurt by withdrawing from the world. It is that anger and disappointment can be transformed into a search for something higher.

His determination shows the power of focused effort. His transformation shows that devotion can change what a person truly wants.

## The deeper meaning

Dhruva is remembered in the tradition through the Dhruva Nakshatra, the Pole Star. His name has become a symbol of steadiness. The story asks devotees to make their faith steady even when circumstances are not.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why did Dhruva go to the forest?

He was hurt by his stepmother's words and, guided by his mother and Narada, went to seek Lord Vishnu through devotion.

### Did Lord Vishnu appear before Dhruva?

Yes. The Bhagavatam describes the Lord appearing before him after his intense tapasya.

### Why is Dhruva associated with the Pole Star?

The tradition connects Dhruva with the fixed star in the northern sky as a symbol of steadiness.
