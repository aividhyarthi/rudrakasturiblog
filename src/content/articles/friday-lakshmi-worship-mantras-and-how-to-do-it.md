In the Hindu week, Monday belongs to Shiva, Thursday to the guru, and Friday to Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth, grace and well-being and the consort of Vishnu. Friday is when many homes in the Telugu states are swept, sprinkled and decorated with rangoli in her honour.

## What Lakshmi stands for
Lakshmi is more than money. The tradition honours her in eight forms, the Ashtalakshmi: **Adi Lakshmi** (the primal), **Dhana Lakshmi** (wealth), **Dhanya Lakshmi** (grain and food), **Gaja Lakshmi** (power and royal honour), **Santana Lakshmi** (children), **Veera or Dhairya Lakshmi** (courage), **Vijaya Lakshmi** (victory) and **Vidya Lakshmi** (knowledge). A prayer to Lakshmi is for all of these.

## A simple Friday puja
1. **Clean the home**, especially the entrance and the place of worship. Lakshmi is said to dwell where there is cleanliness and order.
2. **Draw a small rangoli** (muggu) at the door.
3. **Light a lamp at dusk**, with ghee or sesame oil, before her picture or a kalasha.
4. **Offer** lotus or red flowers, turmeric and kumkum.
5. **Chant** one of the mantras below, 11 or 108 times.
6. **Offer naivedyam** such as payasam or sweet pongal, and share it with the family.

## Mantras
- *Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah* (ఓం శ్రీం మహాలక్ష్మ్యై నమః): "I bow to Mahalakshmi."
- The **Lakshmi Gayatri**: *Om Mahalakshmyai Cha Vidmahe Vishnupatnyai Cha Dhimahi, Tanno Lakshmih Prachodayat*.
- The opening of the **Mahalakshmi Ashtakam**: *Namastestu Mahamaye Shripithe Surapujite*.

Meanings and more are on our page of [Lakshmi mantras](/mantras/lakshmi/).

## Thoughts on keeping Lakshmi
The older teachers say that Lakshmi stays where there is honest earning, restraint in spending, respect for women and gratitude for food. Cleaning and ritual matter, but the habits matter as much. A prayer for wealth sits best next to a plan to earn it fairly.
