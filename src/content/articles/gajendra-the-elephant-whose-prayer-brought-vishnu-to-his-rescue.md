Gajendra was powerful. He was the leader of a herd and feared by other animals. But when a crocodile caught his leg in the water, his strength could not free him.

The Bhagavatam tells this story as Gajendra Moksha, the liberation of Gajendra, and places a prayer at its centre.

## The elephant caught in the water

Gajendra entered a lake with his herd. As he enjoyed the water, a crocodile seized his leg. He struggled to escape, and the fight continued for a very long time.

The other elephants tried to help, but they could not free him. Gradually, Gajendra's strength began to fade.

## When strength was no longer enough

The story describes a turning point. Gajendra understood that physical power could not save him. He remembered the Supreme Lord and offered a prayer of surrender.

He called upon the One who exists beyond the limits of the world and who protects those who seek refuge. His prayer is known as the Gajendra Stuti.

## Lord Vishnu comes to his rescue

Hearing the call of his devotee, Lord Vishnu arrives and frees Gajendra from the crocodile. The episode is remembered as a powerful example of divine grace.

The Bhagavatam also connects the story with the past lives of Gajendra and the crocodile, showing how the events are part of a larger spiritual account.

## What the story teaches

Gajendra's story is not a rejection of effort. He struggles with all his strength. But when he reaches the limit of what he can do, he surrenders to the Lord.

For devotees, the story teaches that prayer is not only for peaceful moments. It can also become the support we turn to when our own strength is exhausted.

## The deeper meaning

Gajendra Moksha reminds us that the Divine is not distant from the suffering of a devotee. The elephant's prayer becomes a symbol of sincere surrender. It is not the animal's strength but the honesty of his call that gives the story its lasting power.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Gajendra Moksha?

It is the Bhagavatam episode in which Lord Vishnu rescues Gajendra, the elephant king, from a crocodile after hearing his prayer.

### Where is Gajendra's prayer found?

The Gajendra Stuti appears in the Eighth Skandha of the Srimad Bhagavatam.

### What is the lesson of Gajendra Moksha?

The story teaches sincere surrender, faith and the belief that divine grace is available even when human strength reaches its limit.
