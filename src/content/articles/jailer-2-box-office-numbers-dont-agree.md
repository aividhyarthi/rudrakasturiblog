Rajinikanth's Jailer 2 reached cinemas on 15 October 2026 and immediately broke the one thing box-office trackers hate: agreement.

Here is what the trade sources reported, and when.

## The numbers, side by side
- **Day 1, India net:** Sacnilk's later tally is ₹59.70 crore from 16,599 shows, and Outlook reports the same figure. The Week's earlier live number was ₹29.39 crore, which looks like a count taken before the day ended.
- **Day 1, worldwide gross:** about ₹125.25 crore, according to Sacnilk and The Week, with overseas contributing roughly ₹55 crore in early estimates.
- **The record that got away:** Outlook notes that the film fell short of Coolie, which took ₹65 crore net on its first day. Sacnilk also places the overseas opening in the all-time top three for Tamil films, but short of Coolie.
- **Two days in:** Sacnilk puts the worldwide total at around ₹209 crore and its day-two India net estimate at ₹35.60 crore. The CSR Journal reports ₹81.70 crore net in India after two days. Those accounts do not line up.

## Even the headlines disagree
IMDb's day-four report calls the weekend Kollywood's second-biggest opening ever and the highest-grossing Tamil film of 2026. Koimoi's day-four report calls it Kollywood's biggest opening weekend and the highest-grossing South Indian film of 2026. Both cannot be true at the same time.

## What to trust
Sacnilk labels several of its own figures as early estimates. We have not found a figure confirmed by the producers in the reports we reviewed. Before release, Outlook reported a trade expert's forecast of more than ₹200 crore for the first weekend. That was a prediction, not a result.

Our rule: trust numbers that name who measured them and when, treat anything marked "early" as provisional, and wait for the final weekend tally.

## What to watch next
Expect the figures to settle once final weekend numbers are published. We will update this story when they are.
