The Karnataka High Court on Monday struck down provisions of a 2022 state law that increased reservation for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, according to UNI's report of 13 October 2026. Here is what is known.

## What happened
The court struck down the provisions that raised the reservation. The state cabinet was due to discuss the verdict, UNI reported.

## What the government says
Deputy Chief Minister G. Parameshwara said the government would weigh its legal options, including a possible challenge in the Supreme Court. He said it would first consult the Advocate General and the Law Department.

## What we could not confirm
We found no report of the cabinet's final decision. We have one source for this story and have not seen the judgment itself.

## What to watch next
Whether the government files an appeal, and what it does about the reservation that now stands struck down in the meantime. We will update this when the decision is reported.
