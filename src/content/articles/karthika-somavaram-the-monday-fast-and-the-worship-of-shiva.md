In the Telugu states, Mondays are the day of Shiva in any month. But the Mondays of Karthika Masam are different. They are called Karthika Somavaram, and at dawn, temples have lines of women with lamps in their hands.

## The dates in 2026
Karthika Masam runs from 10 November to 8 December 2026. The Mondays in it are:
- **16 November**
- **23 November** (the day before Kartika Pournami)
- **30 November**
- **7 December**

## Why Mondays, why this month?
Monday, *Somavara*, is the day of Soma, the Moon, who wears the crescent on Shiva's head. And Karthika is the month in which, on the full moon, Shiva defeated the three demon cities of Tripurasura. We come to that in [the Tripura Pournami article](/tripura-pournami-the-story-of-the-three-cities-and-dev-deepawali/). Put the day and the month together, and you have the strongest time of the year for Shiva worship.

## How people keep the day
1. **Early bath.** Many devotees bathe before sunrise, in a river or tank if possible.
2. **Lamp at dawn.** A lamp is lit in front of the home shrine and often on the Tulasi plant.
3. **Temple visit.** Devotees go to a Shiva temple for abhishekam, with water, milk or honey, bilva leaves and flowers.
4. **Fast.** Many keep a fast until the evening, with water, fruit or milk, and break it after seeing the lamps or the first star.
5. **Evening lamps.** Lamps are lit in the temple and at home, on the tulasi, at the door and sometimes on the amla tree.
6. **Mantra.** *Om Namah Shivaya*, 108 times. See [Shiva mantras](/mantras/shiva/).
7. **Simple dinner.** A light meal, often without onion and garlic.

## The abhishekam
The water poured over the lingam is a symbol of cooling and calm. Many people bring a pot of water from home, with a few drops of milk or a flower in it. The priest chants the Rudram.

## What to eat
If you fast, the traditional foods are fruit, milk, nuts, sabudana or a light meal of rice and curd in the evening. Many break the fast with a meal that has no onion or garlic.

## For women, for families
In many families, it is the women who observe the Somavaram fast, praying for the well-being of the family. In others, the whole family joins. It is a personal choice. If you cannot go to the temple, a lamp and a mantra at home is complete.

## Who should not fast
Children, elders, pregnant and nursing women, people with diabetes, those on regular medication or anyone unwell should not fast. A light meal and a prayer are fine. Tradition values the devotion, not the hunger.

## Popular temples
[Srisailam](/temples/srisailam-mallikarjuna-temple/), [Vemulawada](/vemulawada-raja-rajeshwara-temple-and-the-kode-mokku/), Draksharamam, Kotappakonda and Keesaragutta draw large crowds. Go early, and check queue arrangements in advance.
