Kerala has a new chief minister, V.D. Satheesan, and a government that has been moving quickly on promises. Here is what is on record from reports of its early weeks.

## The record so far
- **102 seats:** the Congress-led win that, per reports, ended a decade-long LDF rule. The Congress high command named Satheesan chief minister on 14 May.
- **Silver Line:** the cabinet moved to dismantle the semi high-speed rail project and cancel the land acquisition notifications issued for it. The News Minute also reported that cases against protesters are being withdrawn.
- **₹1,000 a month:** the raise announced for school cooking staff.
- **A white paper:** the government said it would issue a white paper on the state's finances.
- **PM SHRI:** Satheesan said no decision has been taken on the Centre's PM SHRI scheme, and that the state would await a legal opinion on an agreement signed by the previous government.

## Why it matters
The Silver Line decision stands out because it undoes a project pursued by the previous government. The white paper will show how much room the new government actually has for spending.

## What we could not confirm
Reports we reviewed did not always carry clear dates for each decision, and we found no October announcements. We will add them as they are confirmed.

## What to watch next
What the white paper says about the state's finances, and whether the PM SHRI question is settled.
