Most people plan as if life will always give them more time. King Khatvanga received a different answer. After helping the devas in battle, he asked for a reward. When he learned how little time remained in his life, he chose to turn his attention towards the Divine.

## A king who helped the devas

The Bhagavatam remembers Khatvanga as a ruler who came to the aid of the devas. After the conflict, they offered him a reward. Instead of asking for wealth, power or a place in heaven, he asked how long he would live.

The answer was difficult: only a short time remained.

## What did he do with the time left?

Khatvanga did not waste the remaining moments in regret. He turned his mind away from worldly attachments and focused on the Supreme. The story is remembered as an example of spiritual urgency and clear priorities.

The important point is not the exact length of time. It is the king's response. When he understood that life was limited, he did not spend his remaining time chasing what he could no longer keep.

## A lesson for everyday life

People often postpone prayer, reflection, forgiveness and meaningful work. They tell themselves they will do these things later, when work becomes lighter or life becomes easier.

Khatvanga's story gently challenges that habit. Spiritual life does not have to wait for retirement, old age or a crisis. A person can begin remembering God while carrying out ordinary duties.

## The deeper meaning

The story is about more than death. It is about attention. What we give our attention to shapes the way we live. Khatvanga chose to give his final attention to the Supreme.

For devotees, his example is a reminder that every day can be used meaningfully. We do not need to know how much time remains to begin living with devotion.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who was King Khatvanga?

### What did Khatvanga ask as his reward?

Do not postpone spiritual reflection. Use the time available with clarity and devotion.
