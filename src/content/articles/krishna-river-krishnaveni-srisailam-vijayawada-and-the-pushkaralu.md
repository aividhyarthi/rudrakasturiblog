Nobody in Vijayawada thinks of the river as scenery. It is a fact of life: the bridges, the ghats, the barrage and the rains that fill it. Look at the route it takes, and you begin to see how many of the region's holy places are on its banks.

## The route
The Krishna, also called Krishnaveni, rises in the Western Ghats near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra. It runs about 1,400 km, through Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, and reaches the Bay of Bengal near Machilipatnam. Its main tributary is the Tungabhadra, which joins it near Alampur.

## A river named for a god
Local belief links the river to Vishnu, with the story that it flows from the body of Vishnu, and that Krishnaveni is his daughter or form. There is also a local legend at Mahabaleshwar that Krishna and the river are one, and that is why the source is a place of pilgrimage.

## Places along the way
- **Mahabaleshwar**, with its old temple and the five rivers that are said to join near the source.
- **Alampur**, in Telangana, where the Tungabhadra meets the Krishna. It has the Jogulamba temple, one of the 18 Maha Shakti Peethas, and the nine Navabrahma temples, from the 7th century.
- **Srisailam**, on the bank, with the Mallikarjuna Jyotirlinga and Bhramaramba Shakti Peetha. The Srisailam dam is on the river, and it is a place of pilgrimage as much as a landmark. See the [Srisailam temple guide](/temples/srisailam-mallikarjuna-temple/).
- **Nagarjuna Sagar**, near the site of the old Buddhist city of Vijayapuri.
- **Amaravati**, in Guntur district, with its ancient Buddhist stupa and the Amareswara temple, one of the Pancharama Kshetras.
- **Vijayawada**, where the Kanaka Durga temple sits on the Indrakeeladri hill beside the river. Devotees take a dip in the river at Durga Ghat before darshan, especially in Navaratri.

## Pushkaralu
Like the Godavari, the Krishna has a Pushkaralu festival, once in twelve years, when Jupiter enters Kanya rashi. It lasts twelve days, with ritual baths and ancestor rites at the ghats. The last was in 2016, which meant a huge effort in Vijayawada, and the next is in 2028.

## The farmers' river
The Prakasam Barrage at Vijayawada, built in the 1950s, is the lifeline of the Krishna delta. Like the Godavari delta, it is some of the best farmland in the state. Many families in the region say, with a smile, that the Krishna is the reason the rice is so good.

## Karthika Masam by the river
In the month of Karthika, women float lamps on rivers, and the Krishna ghats at Vijayawada, Srisailam and Alampur are lit every evening. If you are in the region in November, it is a lovely sight. See the dates in our [Karthika Masam guide](/karthika-masam-2026-dates-deepam-and-fasting/).
