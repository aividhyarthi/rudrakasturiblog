Monday (Somavaram) is the day of Shiva in the Telugu and Tamil country. This Monday, 26 October 2026, is also a Pournami day: the full moon tithi runs until 9:41 AM, a combination of Shiva and Chandra that many find especially suitable for prayer.

## A simple Monday puja
1. **Bathe**, wear clean clothes, preferably white.
2. **Abhishekam** of the Shiva lingam or a picture with water, and with milk if you can, while chanting *Om Namah Shivaya*.
3. **Offer bilva leaves** (three leaflets on one stem), white flowers and a little vibhuti.
4. **Light a lamp** and repeat the Panchakshari 108 times.
5. **Naivedyam:** fruit, milk or sweet pongal.

Many families keep a fast until the evening on Mondays, especially during Karthika Masam, and eat after the evening lamp. Fasting is optional; anyone with a health condition should not fast.

## What Om Namah Shivaya means
The five syllables *na-mah-shi-va-ya* are traditionally linked to the five elements: earth, water, fire, air and space. The sentence itself means "I bow to Shiva", and the name Shiva means "the auspicious one". It is the simplest and most widely chanted of all Shiva mantras, and needs no initiation.

The other well-loved Shiva mantras are the **Maha Mrityunjaya** and the **Shiva Gayatri**, with their meanings, on our page of [Shiva mantras](/mantras/shiva/).

## Why bilva and water
Bilva leaves, with their three leaflets, are said to stand for Shiva's three eyes, or for the three gunas. Water and milk recall the story of the Halahala poison taken by Shiva at the churning of the ocean: cooling water is offered to calm the throat.

## Karthika Somavaram
In the Telugu month of Karthika (10 November to 8 December 2026), each Monday is especially sacred. The dates are 16, 23 and 30 November and 7 December. See the [Karthika Masam guide](/karthika-masam-2026-dates-deepam-and-fasting/). The great Shiva temples such as [Srisailam](/temples/srisailam-mallikarjuna-temple/) see their largest crowds then.
