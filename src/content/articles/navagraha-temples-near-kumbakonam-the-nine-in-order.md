If you have read about the nine planets of Vedic astrology, there is a part of Tamil Nadu where you can visit all nine in one trip. Eight of them are close to Kumbakonam, in the Kaveri delta, and the ninth is near Karaikal.

## The nine
| Planet | Temple | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Surya (Sun) | Suryanar Koil | Near Aduthurai |
| Chandra (Moon) | Kailasanathar, Thingalur | Near Thiruvaiyaru |
| Mangala (Mars) | Vaitheeswaran Koil | Near Sirkazhi |
| Budha (Mercury) | Swetharanyeswarar, Thiruvenkadu | Near Sirkazhi |
| Guru (Jupiter) | Apatsahayeswarar, Alangudi | Near Needamangalam |
| Shukra (Venus) | Agniswarar, Kanjanur | Near Kumbakonam |
| Shani (Saturn) | Dharbaranyeswarar, Thirunallar | Near Karaikal |
| Rahu | Naganathaswamy, Thirunageswaram | Near Kumbakonam |
| Ketu | Naganathar, Keezhperumpallam | Near Poompuhar |

Almost all of them are Shiva temples, with the planet worshipped in a shrine within, or as the deity.

## A little about each
- **Suryanar Koil** is unusual because it is a temple of the Sun with the other eight planets in the same hall.
- **Thirunallar** is the best known, particularly for people going through Saturn's difficult periods. Devotees bathe in the Nala Theertham tank before darshan. The story is that King Nala was freed from the influence of Shani here; we told it in [our karma series](/nala-damayanti-and-kali-karma-and-patience/).
- **Vaitheeswaran Koil** is called the temple of the healer, with the god worshipped as a physician, Vaidyanatha.
- **Thirunageswaram** is known for the Rahu shrine, where milk poured on the image is said to turn blue.
- **Alangudi** is for Guru, and is said to protect from dangers, particularly for travellers.

## How people do the circuit
There is a traditional order, which usually starts with Suryanar Koil, and many pilgrims take the nine in sequence over one or two days. Some also add a visit to the Vinayaka temple at Kumbakonam first. A car and a local guide help, as the temples are scattered among villages and many close in the middle of the day, from around noon to 4 p.m.

## What people ask for
People go for relief from planetary difficulties as they believe them, and for peace of mind. If you do not follow astrology, many visitors still enjoy the circuit simply as a journey through the Kaveri delta with some of the best temple architecture in India nearby, including the Airavatesvara temple at Darasuram.

## A reminder
Planetary periods are part of a belief system, and nobody has to visit a temple for a medical, financial or legal problem. See a doctor, adviser or lawyer first.
