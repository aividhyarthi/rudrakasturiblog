*Nava* means nine and *graha* means one that grasps or influences. The Navagraha are the nine bodies Vedic astrology uses to read a horoscope: seven bodies you can see, and two mathematical points on the Moon's path.

## The nine
| Graha | Planet | Day | What tradition links to it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surya | Sun | Sunday | Self, authority, vitality, father |
| Chandra | Moon | Monday | Mind, mood, mother, comfort |
| Mangala | Mars | Tuesday | Energy, courage, property, siblings |
| Budha | Mercury | Wednesday | Intellect, speech, trade, learning |
| Guru | Jupiter | Thursday | Wisdom, teachers, children, good fortune |
| Shukra | Venus | Friday | Love, arts, comfort, vehicles |
| Shani | Saturn | Saturday | Discipline, delay, karma, service |
| Rahu | North lunar node | | Ambition, obsession, the foreign, sudden change |
| Ketu | South lunar node | | Detachment, spirituality, loss, intuition |

## Rahu and Ketu
Rahu and Ketu are not bodies in the sky. They are the two points where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's path, which is also where eclipses happen. They are always exactly opposite each other and move backwards through the zodiac, taking about eighteen months per sign. Stories about them go back to the churning of the ocean, when the demon Svarbhanu drank the nectar and was cut in two. The daily [Rahu Kalam](/rahu-kalam-today/) is named after Rahu.

## How they are used
- **Birth chart:** the position of each graha in your chart is read for its sign, house and relationships to the others.
- **Transits (gochara):** the daily, weekly and monthly [horoscope](/rasi-phalalu/) is worked out from where the grahas are now relative to your Moon sign. For example, [Saturn's current transit](/sade-sati-2026-which-rashis-are-affected/) affects the whole of Sade Sati.
- **Dashas:** a time-period system that says which graha is "in charge" at different stages of life.

## Worship and temples
Many Hindu temples have a Navagraha shrine with nine deities, often circled nine times. A famous set of nine Navagraha temples lies near Kumbakonam in Tamil Nadu, and many pilgrims visit them in one trip.

## A note on perspective
Astrology is a tradition of belief. The Navagraha are used for guidance and reflection, not as a way to predict or blame events. Health, money and legal matters should be handled with the right professionals first.
