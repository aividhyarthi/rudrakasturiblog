The old Indian way of thinking about the world uses five elements: earth, water, fire, air and space. Five Shiva temples in South India are each tied to one of them. Visiting all five is a pilgrimage of its own.

## The five
| Element | Temple | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Earth (Prithvi) | Ekambareswarar | Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu |
| Water (Appu) | Jambukeswarar | Thiruvanaikaval, near Tiruchirappalli |
| Fire (Tejas) | Arunachaleswarar | Tiruvannamalai, Tamil Nadu |
| Air (Vayu) | Srikalahasti | Srikalahasti, Andhra Pradesh |
| Space (Akasha) | Nataraja | Chidambaram, Tamil Nadu |

## How each shows its element
**Kanchipuram, earth.** The lingam is made of sand, shaped by Parvati, according to the story. The temple has a very old mango tree. Read more in [our guide to Kanchi](/kanchipuram-the-city-of-a-thousand-temples/).

**Thiruvanaikaval, water.** The Jambukeswarar lingam sits in a chamber where water keeps seeping up from the ground, so that the base is always damp. The temple's name comes from an elephant and a spider. The story says that an elephant used to bring water from the river to bathe the lingam, and a spider covered it with web to shelter it from the sun. They fought and died, and both were reborn as a Chola king and a devotee. It is close to Srirangam, so many people visit the two on the same day.

**Tiruvannamalai, fire.** The lingam is the fire pillar, and the whole hill of Arunachala is the fire. On the night of Karthigai Deepam, a huge lamp is lit on top of the hill, visible for many kilometres. Pilgrims walk around the hill, a route of about 14 km called Girivalam, on full moon nights.

**Srikalahasti, air.** This is the one in Andhra Pradesh, in Tirupati district. The lingam is said to be where the air-god Vayu is worshipped. The sign that visitors are shown is a lamp inside the sanctum, which flickers constantly even though there is no wind in the closed room. The name comes from a spider (sri), a snake (kala) and an elephant (hasti), who all worshipped Shiva here and attained liberation, according to the story. Srikalahasti is also well known for the Rahu Ketu pooja, performed by people who believe they are affected by those planets, and for Kalamkari art.

**Chidambaram, space.** The Nataraja temple, with its empty space behind a curtain, is the element of space. We wrote about it in [the Chidambaram guide](/chidambaram-nataraja-temple-the-temple-of-space/).

## Do they have to be done in order?
There is no fixed rule, though many people go in the order of the elements as they were just listed: earth, water, fire, air and space. If you are in Andhra, Srikalahasti is close to Tirupati and Tirumala, so it is common to combine it with a trip to Balaji.

## The idea behind it
The temples remind a visitor that the divine is not separate from the world. The ground you walk on, the water you drink, the fire that cooks, the air and the sky around you are all part of the same thing. It matches the teaching of the Upanishads that we looked at in [our Upanishad map](/the-ten-principal-upanishads-a-beginners-map/).
