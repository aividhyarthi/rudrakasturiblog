Ranabaali, the period action drama starring Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna, opened in cinemas on 16 October 2026. The first-day number depends on what time you looked.

## Day one, as the day went on
- **Early live tally (Sacnilk):** about ₹1.68 crore net from the Telugu version across 736 shows. That page said final numbers were still to come.
- **Mid-day (GoodReturns, using Sacnilk data):** ₹3.87 crore net from Telugu shows, about ₹4.57 crore India gross. Hindi occupancy was weak at 8%.
- **Later total (The CSR Journal, citing Sacnilk):** ₹7.76 crore for the day, of which about ₹7.75 crore came from the Telugu version.

Same film, same day, three numbers. That is how live box-office tracking works.

## The mood
The CSR Journal called the opening lukewarm and said it trailed Deverakonda's earlier film Kingdom. GoodReturns described Ranabaali as a period action drama with sincere effort but moderate impact. NewsBytes had earlier reported on its advance booking figures.

## What we could not confirm
We found no verified multi-day total for Ranabaali. Day-one figures from trackers are provisional, and we have not found a producer-announced number. We will update this story when firmer figures appear.

## What to watch next
The first weekend usually shows whether a film has legs. Watch the day-three and day-four totals, and whether the Hindi version picks up.
