Telangana's Congress government crossed 1,000 days in office in early September 2026. Both sides produced scorecards. Here is whose claim is whose.

## What the government's side says
- TPCC working president T. Jaggareddy credited the government with welfare schemes: free bus travel for women, 250 units of free electricity, LPG cylinders at ₹500, and new ration cards and Indiramma houses.
- Chief Minister Revanth Reddy has said per-capita income reached ₹4,18,931 in 2025-26 and that ₹1.29 lakh crore in investments were secured through TG-iPASS.
- In July, he said 70,000 government jobs had been filled and recruitment for 11,000 teacher posts completed.

## What critics say
- The BJP called the 1,000 days a "complete failure", according to IANS.
- The BRS opposition is preparing a statewide campaign to frame the period as betrayal of promises, per the reports we reviewed.

## The receipts problem
Every figure above is a claim by the government or a political party. We have not independently checked any of them. Per-capita income, investment commitments and job numbers each have official sources, such as the state budget and departmental data, that can confirm or challenge them.

## What to watch next
We will test the headline numbers against official documents and publish what we find. Neither the government nor its critics has yet offered a side-by-side comparison with manifesto promises.
