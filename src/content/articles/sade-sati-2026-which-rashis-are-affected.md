Sade Sati is one of the most searched ideas in Vedic astrology. It is worth understanding calmly, because it is often described in frightening terms that traditional texts do not support.

## What Sade Sati is
*Sade Sati* means seven and a half years. It is the period when Saturn (Shani) passes through three signs in a row: the sign before your rashi, your rashi itself, and the sign after it. Saturn spends about two and a half years in each sign, so the three phases add up to about seven and a half years.

Your rashi here means your **janma rashi**, the sign your Moon was in at birth, not your Western Sun sign. If you are not sure of it, read [how to find your janma rashi](/find-your-janma-rashi-moon-sign/).

## Which rashis are in Sade Sati now
Saturn entered Meena (Pisces) on 29 March 2025 and moves into Mesha (Aries) in early June 2027. During that time:

- **Mesha (Aries):** first phase. Saturn is in the 12th sign from the Moon.
- **Meena (Pisces):** middle phase. Saturn is over the Moon sign itself, which traditions call the most intense stretch.
- **Kumbha (Aquarius):** last phase. Saturn is in the 2nd sign from the Moon.

Some traditions also speak of Ashtama Shani, when Saturn is in the 8th sign from the Moon (**Simha**, Leo), and Ardhashtama Shani, the 4th sign (**Dhanu**, Sagittarius). Not every astrologer counts these, and they carry less weight than Sade Sati.

## For other rashis
Under the classical gochara (transit) rules, Saturn in Meena is considered favourable for **Vrishabha, Tula and Makara** rashis, because it sits in their 11th, 6th and 3rd signs from the Moon. Other rashis see mixed results. Read your daily and weekly horoscope on the [rasi phalalu page](/rasi-phalalu/) to see how this plays out week to week.

## What the texts and tradition actually say
Saturn is the planet of discipline, time and consequences. Traditional writers describe Sade Sati as a period that slows things down and asks for patience, effort and honesty, not as a curse. Results are said to depend on the whole horoscope, including the strength of Saturn in your birth chart and the running dasha. Many people report their steadiest growth during Sade Sati.

## What people traditionally do
- Worship on Saturdays: lighting a sesame oil lamp, visiting a Hanuman, Shani or Venkateswara temple.
- Reading the Hanuman Chalisa or Shani Chalisa.
- Giving to those in need: food, black sesame, shoes or umbrellas.
- Living with discipline: regular routine, keeping promises, respecting elders and workers.

## What not to do
Do not make big financial or health decisions out of fear because of an astrological period, and do not pay anyone who promises to "remove" Sade Sati. If you have a health, money or legal problem, see a doctor, a financial adviser or a lawyer first.
