Sankashti Chaturthi, also called Sankata Hara Chaturthi, is the Chaturthi of the dark fortnight, observed every month in honour of Ganesha. *Sankashti* means deliverance from difficulty.

## Date and timings (Hyderabad)
- **Date:** Thursday, 29 October 2026.
- **Krishna Chaturthi tithi:** present at sunrise on 29 October and ends at 10:10 PM.
- **Moonrise:** about 8:36 PM.

Since the tithi is in force at moonrise, the fast is kept on this day and broken after the Moon is seen. Moonrise differs by a few minutes between cities, so check the [panchangam for your location](/panchangam/).

In north India the same day is Karwa Chauth.

## How to observe it
1. **Fast through the day.** Many people take only fruit and water, or eat nothing until moonrise. A lighter meal is fine for those who need it.
2. **Evening Ganesha puja.** Light a lamp, offer durva grass, red hibiscus and a naivedyam of modak or laddu.
3. **Chant.** *Om Gam Ganapataye Namah* (ఓం గం గణపతయే నమః) 108 times, or *Vakratunda Mahakaya*. Meanings are on our [Ganesha mantras page](/mantras/ganesha/).
4. **After moonrise,** offer a little water (arghya) to the Moon, then break the fast with the prasad.

## Why the Moon
Ganesha cursed the Moon for laughing at him, and later relented. The Moon is therefore offered respect on this night. Whatever one makes of the story, the practical rhythm is a day of restraint ending with a calm look at the sky.

## When it falls on a Tuesday
If Sankashti Chaturthi falls on a Tuesday, it is called Angaraki Chaturthi and is considered especially powerful. This month it falls on a Thursday.

## Sankashti and Vinayaka Chaturthi
The Chaturthi of the dark fortnight is Sankashti, and the Chaturthi of the bright fortnight is Vinayaka Chaturthi. Both are Ganesha's days, and the annual festival, Vinayaka Chavithi, falls in the month of Bhadrapada.

## What to do on the day
Here is a simple way to arrange the day, using the timings above.
- **Morning.** Have a bath, light a lamp before Ganesha and take your sankalpa, a quiet intention such as "I will keep this fast with devotion today". Begin your fast in the way that suits your health.
- **Through the day.** Keep your mind light. A few rounds of *Om Gam Ganapataye Namah* during the day help the hours pass gently.
- **Evening.** Clean the puja area and prepare the offerings before moonrise, which is about 8:36 PM in Hyderabad.
- **After moonrise.** Complete the puja, offer arghya to the Moon, and break the fast with prasad and a simple meal.
- **Before bed.** Thank Ganesha, put the lamp out safely and rest.

## Moonrise puja steps
1. Place a clean cloth on a stool and set Ganesha's image or idol on it.
2. Offer a flower, a few blades of durva grass and a little kumkum.
3. Light a lamp and an incense stick, if you use them.
4. Offer the naivedyam, such as modak, laddu or a fruit.
5. Chant *Om Gam Ganapataye Namah* 108 times, or read the Sankashti story if your family has one.
6. When the Moon appears, offer a little water from a small vessel with folded hands and a short prayer.
7. Take the prasad and break the fast.

## Questions people ask

**What if the Moon is hidden by clouds?**
Many families go ahead and offer the arghya in the direction of the Moon at the stated moonrise time, then break the fast. Follow your family's custom.

**Why durva grass?**
Durva is considered dear to Ganesha. A Puranic story tells that Ganesha swallowed a troublesome demon called Analasura and was cooled by durva offered to him. It is a tradition, and different texts tell it differently.

**Can I keep the fast with a meal?**
Yes. Fasting is optional, and many families keep a lighter form with fruit and milk. Children, elders, pregnant and nursing women, and anyone with a health condition or on regular medication should not fast strictly. Devotion matters more than hunger.

**Is Sankashti only for solving problems?**
It is also a monthly pause. Many people say the greatest value is the quiet hour at the end of the day.

**What if I cannot be home at moonrise?**
You can do the puja earlier in the evening and offer the arghya when you see the Moon later.

## A gentle note on keeping a vrat
A fast is meant to make you lighter, not harder on yourself. If you feel faint, headachy or unwell, stop and eat something. Drink water through the day unless your doctor has told you otherwise. Keep your voice kind with the people at home, because a patient word is part of the offering. If this is your first Sankashti, start with a half-day fast and see how you feel before you try a full day.
