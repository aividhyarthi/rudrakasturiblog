On a Friday evening in many South Indian homes, the house is swept, the threshold is washed, a kolam is drawn fresh, and a lamp is lit in front of the goddess. The house smells of jasmine and ghee. Nobody calls it astrology. It is simply how Friday feels. And in the nine-planet tradition, this gentle, well-kept, comfortable Friday belongs to Shukra.

Shukra, called Sukran or Velli in Tamil and Shukrudu in Telugu (శుక్రుడు), is the planet Venus, the sixth in the common order of the nine. For the full set, start with our overview of [the Navagrahas in Vedic astrology](/navagraha-the-nine-planets-in-vedic-astrology/).

## What Shukra is said to govern

In the symbolic language of astrology, Venus is associated with:

- love, affection and married life
- comfort, home and the pleasures of living well
- beauty, art, music and dance
- refinement, grace and the appreciation of good things

You will see that this is a very human list. Tradition does not frown on comfort. It asks only that comfort be balanced with dharma, which is the old way of saying that enjoyment is fine when it does not cost someone else their peace.

As with every graha, these are themes and not guarantees. A strong Venus in a chart does not promise a happy marriage, and a weak one does not forbid it. A relationship is built by two people, in daily acts of patience, and no planet does that work for them.

## Shukracharya and the Sanjivani vidya

The Puranas tell the story of Shukracharya, the guru of the asuras, who is also identified with the planet. Tradition holds that he knew the Sanjivani vidya, a knowledge that could bring the dead back to life. In the wars between devas and asuras, he used it to revive the fallen asuras, which troubled the gods.

The tale goes on to describe how Kacha, the son of Brihaspati, was sent to learn this knowledge from Shukracharya, and how the story ends in sorrow and in a curse. Versions differ, and the details are told differently in different Puranas and regions, so it is best to hear it from a reliable storyteller in your own tradition. The teaching most people draw from it is gentle: knowledge is powerful, and the one who holds it has a duty to use it fairly.

## Day, colour, grain and mantra

What families commonly associate with Shukra:

- **Day:** Friday, Shukravaram in Telugu, Velli-k-kizhamai in Tamil.
- **Colour:** white, or cream and pale pastel shades.
- **Grain:** white beans such as mochai or avarai (field beans), or white lentils in some regions.
- **Flower and gem:** white flowers, jasmine and white lotus; diamond is traditionally named, though it is absolutely not required.
- **Mantra:** Om Shukraya Namah.
- **Deity linked with him:** Lakshmi, and in some traditions Parvati and Durga.

## Friday and Lakshmi

Friday is, in most South Indian homes, the day of the Goddess. The lamp, the kolam, the flowers, the sweet made at home: this is how many families keep Friday. Our article on [Friday Lakshmi worship, mantras and how to do it](/friday-lakshmi-worship-mantras-and-how-to-do-it/) explains the steps for an ordinary household.

A word of honesty: praying to Lakshmi on Friday is a devotion, not a business arrangement. The tradition speaks of Lakshmi as grace, shri, more than as money. Many elders say a home becomes wealthy first in peace and kindness, and only then in anything else. Please be cautious of anyone who promises riches in return for a payment or a special puja.

Ask yourself, too, what comfort means to you. Is it a soft bed, a full table, a quiet evening with people you trust? Venus is not asking you to give these up. She is asking whether you notice them, and whether you give thanks for them before they are gone.

## The Shukra sthalam

Among the nine Navagraha temples near Kumbakonam, the one for Shukra is Kanjanur, with the Agneeswarar temple. Tradition holds that Shiva here is worshipped as Agneeswarar, and devotees traditionally come on Fridays. It is often visited as part of the pilgrimage described in our guide to [the Navagraha temples near Kumbakonam in order](/navagraha-temples-near-kumbakonam-the-nine-in-order/).

## A word to those who worry about their charts

Some people find that their Venus is described as weak or afflicted in their horoscope, and they begin to worry about love or marriage. If this is you, take a breath. Astrologers differ widely in how they read Venus, and a single placement says very little by itself. No one should tell you that your relationships are doomed or that you must buy an expensive stone to fix them. A kind conversation, a patient heart and a trusted elder to talk to will take you farther.

## A simple practice for Friday

Here is a way to keep Friday that costs nothing.

1. Tidy one corner of your home, even a small shelf, and wash your hands and feet.
2. Light a lamp, and if you have it, put one white flower before it.
3. Say Om Shukraya Namah, or your family's Lakshmi mantra, eleven times.
4. Do one small act of care for someone close to you: cook something they like, or write them a few kind lines.

Venus, in the end, is about how we love the people near us. If you do that one thing, you have worshipped well.
