Here is where this week's South releases landed, according to weekly OTT roundups from Sacnilk and Boldsky. We have not watched or reviewed them, so treat this as a map, not a verdict.

## The five
- **The Traitors (Telugu reality competition):** Prime Video, 15 October.
- **Enna Vilai (Tamil drama):** released 15 October.
- **Kappi Squad (Malayalam comedy-drama):** JioHotstar, 15 October.
- **Aasha (mystery-thriller):** ZEE5, 16 October, with Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada audio.
- **DC:** SonyLIV, 16 October, in Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Hindi.

## Planning your weekend
Aasha and DC are the easy picks if you care about language choice, since both offer multiple audio tracks. If you want something to argue about with friends, The Traitors is the one built for it.

## One caution
Release dates on OTT can shift, and the roundups we used do not always agree. Check the platform before you plan a binge.
