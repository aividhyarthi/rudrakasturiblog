Before Vali leaves to face Sugriva, Tara asks him to stop and think. She has noticed that Sugriva has returned with unusual confidence. She suspects that he may have found a powerful ally. Vali does not listen.

The Ramayana remembers Tara as more than a queen mourning her husband. She is a woman whose judgement is sharp, whose warning is clear and whose advice is ignored.

## Sugriva returns to challenge Vali

After the first encounter between the brothers, Sugriva returns and calls Vali out again. Tara notices that something is different. Sugriva, who had earlier been defeated, now appears confident enough to challenge Vali once more.

Tara asks Vali to consider why this has happened. She has heard that Sugriva may have formed an alliance with powerful outsiders. She advises Vali not to rush into the fight.

## A warning that pride refuses to hear

Vali is confident in his strength. He believes that no one can defeat him. He dismisses Tara's concern and goes out to meet Sugriva.

The encounter ends with Rama's intervention and Vali's death. Tara's warning becomes painful because the danger she sensed was real.

## Tara's strength after the loss

Tara's role does not end with the warning. After Vali falls, she grieves and questions Rama about the justice of what has happened. Her words show both her love for Vali and her ability to speak directly even in a moment of great sorrow.

The Ramayana presents her as intelligent, politically aware and emotionally strong.

## What her story teaches

Strength is useful, but it can become dangerous when it turns into overconfidence. Tara's story reminds us that listening is also a form of wisdom. A warning should not be rejected simply because it comes from someone who does not share our pride.

## The deeper meaning

Tara sees what Vali refuses to see. Her story asks us to recognise the value of wise counsel before a crisis becomes irreversible. In family life, leadership and public life, the ability to listen can protect us from mistakes that strength alone cannot prevent.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who was Tara in the Ramayana?

Tara was Vali's wife and the queen of Kishkindha, known for her wisdom and sound judgement.

### What did Tara warn Vali about?

She warned him that Sugriva's renewed confidence suggested that he had gained powerful support and that Vali should not rush into battle.

### Did Vali listen to Tara?

No. He ignored her warning and went out to fight Sugriva.
