Five months into the TVK era, here are the numbers that define Tamil Nadu's newest government, drawn from reports around the May 2026 swearing-in.

## The numbers
- **108 of 234:** seats won by Vijay's Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) on its electoral debut, according to reports. The Assembly's majority mark is 118.
- **120 or 121:** MLAs reported to be backing the government once the Congress and several smaller parties came on board. Reports differ on the exact count.
- **10 May 2026:** the day Governor Rajendra Arlekar administered the oath to Vijay and nine other ministers at Nehru Stadium in Chennai.
- **Three:** the first measures Vijay was reported to have signed off on: 200 units of free power supply, a special task force on drugs, and a special force for women's protection.

## Why it matters
A first-time party forming a government is rare anywhere. In Tamil Nadu it also changes the usual arithmetic between the established Dravidian parties. The government's strength depends on partners, which is worth remembering whenever a big decision comes up.

## What we could not confirm
These figures come from May 2026 coverage. We have not independently checked the Assembly's current strength, and the two support counts reported above differ.

## What to watch next
Whether the free-power and task-force announcements show up as measurable results, and how the alliance holds as decisions get harder.
