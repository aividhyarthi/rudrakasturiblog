This is a delicate subject. For a couple who want a child and are waiting, every Sunday lunch with relatives, every baby announcement and every careless question can sting. If this is you, this article is written gently.

## First, the medical part
In many cases, delay in having a child has a cause that can be found and treated, and it can lie with either partner. Doctors usually say that a couple who have tried for a year without success, or six months if the woman is over 35, should see a specialist. Do not delay out of shame. And please do not let anyone blame only the woman.

## The gods people pray to
### Santana Gopala Krishna
*Santana* means offspring and *Gopala* is Krishna the cowherd. A form of the child Krishna is worshipped by couples hoping for a child.

*Devaki Sutam Govindam Vasudeva Jagatpate,*
*Dehi Me Tanayam Krishna Tvam Aham Sharanam Gatah.*
*Meaning:* O Govinda, son of Devaki, lord of the world, son of Vasudeva, give me a child. I have taken refuge in you.

The usual practice is to say the verse 108 times a day. You can read about the god in the [Krishna guide](/krishna-the-cowherd-the-king-and-the-teacher/) and see [Guruvayur](/guruvayur-the-temple-where-babies-eat-their-first-rice/), where many families go.

### Subramanya
In the Telugu and Tamil country, many couples pray to Subramanya and visit his temples on Shashthi. Some also worship snakes, in the belief that they relate to childlessness. See the notes on beliefs in [Kukke Subramanya](/kukke-subramanya-temple-sarpa-dosha-and-the-snake-worship-of-the-south/). *Om Saravanabhavaya Namah* is the short mantra, on the [Subramanya page](/mantras/subramanya/).

### Shashthi
The sixth tithi is associated with the protection of children. In some parts of India, mothers observe a fast and prayers on Shashthi for their children's health.

## A simple routine
1. **Pick a time each day,** morning or evening.
2. **Light a lamp** and say the verse, 11 or 108 times.
3. **Sit together** if both partners are willing. It brings you closer.
4. **Give something:** feed cows, give milk to children, or donate to an orphanage.

## Take care of each other
The wait can strain a marriage. A few things help:
- **Talk about it,** and don't let it become a silent shame.
- **Don't blame.** It is a shared journey.
- **Protect yourselves from comments.** Set a line with relatives who ask, politely.
- **Look after your health.** Sleep, food and exercise matter for fertility, too.
- **Think about options,** from treatment to adoption, in your own time.

## What to avoid
- Anyone who promises a child through a ritual in exchange for a fee.
- Unregulated clinics and unproven remedies.
- Guilt. You have done nothing wrong.

## A last thought
The Gita says you have a right to your effort but not to the fruits of it (see [Gita 2.47](/bhagavad-gita-2-47-meaning-karmanye-vadhikaraste/)). In this case, effort means medical care, patience and prayer. The rest is not in your control, and that is nobody's fault.

If you feel very low, speak to a counsellor. In India, Tele-MANAS (14416) is free and open at all hours.

*Prayer brings calm and hope, and many people find it a real strength. It works best alongside practical steps. No mantra guarantees an outcome, and no one should pay large sums to anyone who promises one.*
