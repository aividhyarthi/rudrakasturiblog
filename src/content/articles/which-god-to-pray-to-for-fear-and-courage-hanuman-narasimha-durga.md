A job interview. A dark corridor. A surgery. A conflict with someone more powerful. The mouth goes dry and the heart pounds. People through the ages have turned to three gods in such moments.

## Hanuman
Hanuman is the monkey god who leapt across the ocean, and he is strength and courage in the service of a good cause. Tuesdays and Saturdays are his days.

**A verse said for courage**
*Buddhir Balam Yasho Dhairyam Nirbhayatvam Arogata,*
*Ajadyam Vak Patutvam Cha Hanumat Smaranad Bhavet.*
*Meaning:* Intelligence, strength, fame, courage, fearlessness, health, freedom from dullness and eloquence come from remembering Hanuman.

The *Hanuman Chalisa* is the most popular prayer. See [the meaning of the first verses](/hanuman-chalisa-meaning-first-five-verses/). More on the [Hanuman mantras page](/mantras/hanuman/).

## Narasimha
Narasimha, the man-lion, burst from a pillar to protect his child devotee Prahlada from his father. He stands for the divine that arrives in the moment of greatest fear.

*Ugram Viram Maha Vishnum Jvalantam Sarvato Mukham,*
*Nrisimham Bhishanam Bhadram Mrityu Mrityum Namamyaham.*
*Meaning:* I bow to Narasimha, fierce and heroic, the great Vishnu, blazing and facing every direction, terrible yet gracious, the death of death.

Temples: Yadagirigutta, Ahobilam, Mangalagiri, Simhachalam. See [the Andhra hill temples](/hills-of-andhra-where-the-gods-live-simhachalam-ahobilam-srisailam/).

## Durga
Durga rides a lion and carries weapons in each of her hands. She stands for the protective mother. The verse from the Devi Mahatmya is:

*Durge Smrita Harasi Bhitim Asheshajantoh,*
*Svasthaih Smrita Matim Ativa Shubham Dadasi.*
*Meaning:* O Durga, when remembered, you remove the fear of all beings. When remembered by those at peace, you give a most auspicious mind.

Friday and Tuesday are her days, and Navaratri is her festival. See [Durga mantras](/mantras/durga/).

## How to use these
1. **Choose one** that you feel close to.
2. **Say the verse slowly,** once or three times, before the frightening event.
3. **Breathe out slowly** after each.
4. **Remember that the verse steadies you,** it does not remove the thing you fear.

## Practical steps
- **Prepare.** Fear drops when you have done what you can.
- **Break the task into a first step.** Do just that.
- **Rehearse.** For an interview or a talk, practise out loud.
- **Slow your breath.** Longer out than in.
- **Tell someone.** Fear shrinks when shared.

## What Krishna says
In the Gita, Krishna says that even a little practice protects from great fear. See [the Gita on fear](/gita-on-fear-even-a-little-practice-protects-you/).

## When it doesn't go away
Constant worry, panic attacks, avoiding places or people, or a feeling of dread that does not leave can be signs of an anxiety disorder, which is common and treatable. Please see a doctor or counsellor, or call Tele-MANAS on 14416.

*Prayer brings calm and hope, and many people find it a real strength. It works best alongside practical steps. No mantra guarantees an outcome, and no one should pay large sums to anyone who promises one.*
