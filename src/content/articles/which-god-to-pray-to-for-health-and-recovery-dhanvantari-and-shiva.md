When someone you love is ill, the first thing you do is call the doctor. The second, in a Hindu home, is often to light a lamp. Both are right. Here are the gods that people turn to in illness, and what to say.

## Dhanvantari: the physician of the gods
In the story of the churning of the ocean, Dhanvantari rose from the waves carrying a pot of nectar, *amrita*. He is regarded as the divine physician and the source of Ayurveda. His festival, Dhanteras, falls two days before Deepavali (6 November in 2026). See the [Deepavali guide](/deepavali-2026-dates-dhanteras-naraka-chaturdashi-lakshmi-puja/).

**The mantra**
*Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya Dhanvantaraye Amrita Kalasha Hastaya Sarva Amaya Vinashanaya Trailokya Nathaya Sri Maha Vishnave Swaha.*
*Meaning:* Salutations to the Lord Vasudeva, Dhanvantari, who holds the pot of nectar, destroyer of all diseases, lord of the three worlds, Vishnu.

## Shiva as Mrityunjaya
*Mrityunjaya* means the conqueror of death. The Maha Mrityunjaya mantra is the best-known prayer for health and fearlessness. It appears in the Rig Veda.

*Om Tryambakam Yajamahe Sugandhim Pushtivardhanam,*
*Urvarukamiva Bandhanan Mrityor Mukshiya Mamritat.*
*Meaning:* We worship the three-eyed Lord, fragrant and nourishing. As a ripe cucumber is freed from its vine, may he free us from the bondage of death, and not from immortality.

It is chanted 108 times, often for another person. See all the [Shiva mantras](/mantras/shiva/).

## Other helpers
- **Surya.** For vitality, the Surya Gayatri at sunrise. See [Aditya Hridayam](/aditya-hridayam-sunday-surya-worship-and-meaning/).
- **Hanuman,** the one who brought the healing herb, the Sanjeevani, for Lakshmana. A prayer to him is common for recovery and strength.
- **Temples.** [Vaitheeswaran Koil](/vaitheeswaran-koil-the-temple-of-the-divine-healer/) in Tamil Nadu is the best-known temple of the healer.

## If someone is in hospital
1. **Let the doctors do their work.** Follow the treatment.
2. **Chant at a quiet time,** at home or in a corner of the hospital, at a volume that doesn't disturb.
3. **Offer a lamp at home** each evening.
4. **Give something in the patient's name:** food, a donation or a small act of service.
5. **Look after yourself.** Caregivers burn out. Eat, sleep and ask for help.

## What to avoid
- Don't stop medicine because of a vow.
- Don't give a patient unproven remedies on the advice of a ritual specialist.
- Don't pay large sums to anyone claiming to guarantee a cure.
- Don't let guilt creep in. Illness is not a punishment, and nobody deserves it.

## What prayer does
Many patients say prayer gave them calm and courage, and many families say it gave them a way of doing something when they felt helpless. That is real, and valuable. But it is a companion to treatment, not a rival.

For the other side, see [the Gita on grief](/gita-on-grief-what-krishna-says-to-someone-who-has-lost-someone/) and [yoga for anxiety](/yoga-for-anxiety-and-stress-a-ten-minute-reset/).

*Prayer brings calm and hope, and many people find it a real strength. It works best alongside practical steps. No mantra guarantees an outcome, and no one should pay large sums to anyone who promises one.*
