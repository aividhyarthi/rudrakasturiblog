Few things worry a South Indian family more than a delayed wedding. Relatives ask, horoscopes are matched and rematched, and a young person starts to feel like a problem to be solved. Here are the gods that tradition turns to, and some sane advice.

## Katyayani
The Bhagavata Purana tells that the young women of Vrindavan, the gopis, kept a month-long vow in winter to worship the goddess Katyayani, asking that Krishna be their husband. That is the origin of the practice, and unmarried girls have prayed to her for centuries.

*Katyayani Mahamaye Mahayoginyadhishvari,*
*Nandagopasutam Devi Patim Me Kuru Te Namah.*
*Meaning (as the gopis said it):* O Katyayani, great illusion, great yogini, mistress of all, make the son of Nandagopa my husband. Salutations to you.

In practice, people replace the line with the wish for a good life partner. See our [Durga page](/mantras/durga/) for the goddess.

## Gauri and Shankara
A very common prayer is to Parvati, as she won Shiva through penance.
*Hey Gauri Shankararthangi Yatha Tvam Shankara Priya,*
*Tatha Mam Kuru Kalyani Kanta Kantam Sudurlabham.*
*Meaning:* O Gauri, who shares half of Shankara, as you are dear to Shankara, make me, O auspicious one, blessed with a husband who is hard to find.

Monday is the day. See [Shiva mantras](/mantras/shiva/) and the [Karthika Somavaram guide](/karthika-somavaram-the-monday-fast-and-the-worship-of-shiva/).

## Sita and Rama
The divine couple is the model of marriage in Telugu homes. On Sri Rama Navami, the wedding is celebrated at [Bhadrachalam](/bhadrachalam-sri-rama-navami-kalyanam-and-the-story-of-ramadasu/), and many families pray there for good marriages.

## A simple weekly practice
1. **Monday or Friday morning,** light a lamp.
2. **Say the verse** once, with a flower.
3. **Spend a minute** thinking of the kind of person and home you hope for. Be specific about character, not looks.
4. **End with thanks.**

## What goes with the prayer
- **Do not accept pressure.** Your life is not a failure at a particular age.
- **Be clear about what matters.** Values, kindness, health, how you will handle disagreement.
- **Meet people.** Through family, friends, community events, work. Prayer is better when you also go out.
- **Take your time with a decision.** A bad marriage is worse than a late one.
- **Be careful with fear about horoscopes.** Astrology is a belief, and no one should be told that they cannot marry because of a planetary position. If you find someone good, the match matters more than a chart.
- **Be careful with money.** Do not pay someone who promises to get you married through a ritual.

## For parents
Keep communication open. Ask your child what they want, and listen. A child who feels safe will tell you.

## If you are feeling low about it
Waiting can be painful, and loneliness is real. Talk to a friend, and if the feeling lingers, to a counsellor. See [yoga when you feel low](/yoga-when-you-feel-low-small-practices-for-a-heavy-mood/).

## A closer look at Parvati's penance
In the Puranas, Parvati did not simply wait for Shiva to notice her. She went to the forest and practised long austerities to win him. The Gauri prayer above remembers that story. What many women take from it is not the idea of waiting for a husband to appear, but the idea of becoming steady, patient and clear about what one wants. Her prayer is about inner strength first.

Another marriage story you may enjoy is that of [Meenakshi of Madurai](/meenakshi-of-madurai-the-warrior-queen-who-met-shiva/), a bride who was also a queen, and whose wedding to Shiva is still celebrated in Madurai every year.

## Places families visit
Some families visit particular temples when a wedding is awaited. Thirumanancheri, near Mayiladuthurai in Tamil Nadu, is widely known as a temple of marriage prayers. Bhadrachalam, mentioned above, is another, especially around Sri Rama Navami. Many families simply go to their own kuladevata or the nearest Shiva or Devi temple. Where you pray matters less than the steadiness with which you go.

## Questions people ask
**Is it all right for a man to use these prayers?**
Yes. The Sita and Rama prayer is especially popular with both sons and daughters, and many young men pray to Parvati and Shiva for a good partner.

**What if the wait lasts for years?**
Many people marry later than expected and are happy. A delay is not a verdict on you. Keep living your life: study, work, friendships, service.

**Should I fast?**
Some families keep a Monday fast. If you do, keep it gentle and eat enough. This is not medical advice, and anyone with a health condition should not fast without their doctor's guidance.

**Can parents pray on behalf of a child?**
Yes. Many parents light a lamp and offer a verse every week. Just make sure you also talk with your child, and do not turn the prayer into pressure.
