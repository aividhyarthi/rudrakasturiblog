Debt is one of the heaviest things a person can carry. It affects sleep, marriages, health and self-respect. Many of us have a relative who quietly asked the family priest what to do. Here is what the tradition offers, and what must go alongside.

## Lakshmi
Lakshmi is the goddess of wealth and well-being, and her day is Friday. She is more than money. She stands for the stability that comes from honest work and care. Read about her day in [Friday Lakshmi worship](/friday-lakshmi-worship-mantras-and-how-to-do-it/).

*Om Shreem Mahalakshmyai Namah* (ఓం శ్రీం మహాలక్ష్మ్యై నమః). 108 times on Fridays.

## Kubera
Kubera is the lord of wealth and the treasurer of the gods. In the Balaji story, he lent the money for the wedding, as we describe in [the Tirumala guide](/balaji-venkateswara-of-tirumala-seven-hills-one-lord/). Many people pray to him at Deepavali.

*Om Yakshaya Kuberaya Vaishravanaya Dhanadhanyadhipataye, Dhanadhanya Samriddhim Me Dehi Dapaya Svaha.*
*Meaning:* Salutations to Kubera, the lord of wealth and grain. Give me prosperity of wealth and grain.

## Lakshmi Narasimha: release from debt
The form most often prayed to for debt is **Narasimha**, the man-lion, with Lakshmi seated on his lap. The prayer is called *Rina Vimochana Narasimha*, the one who frees from debt.

*Devata Karya Siddhyartham Sabhastambha Samudbhavam,*
*Sri Narasimham Maha Viram Namami Rina Muktaye.*
*Meaning:* To fulfil the work of the gods, he emerged from a pillar in the assembly hall. I bow to the great hero Sri Narasimha, for release from debt.

It is chanted on Saturdays, Swati nakshatra days and Chaturdashi. Yadagirigutta in Telangana and Ahobilam in Andhra are Narasimha's well-known temples. See [the Andhra hill temples](/hills-of-andhra-where-the-gods-live-simhachalam-ahobilam-srisailam/).

## What goes with the prayer
A mantra does not erase a loan. The money has to be repaid, and here are the steps that actually work.
1. **Write everything down.** Every loan, the amount, the interest and the minimum payment. Many people avoid this because it is frightening. It is the first step.
2. **Make a simple budget.** Income, essentials, repayments. Cut what you can.
3. **Stop new debt.** Do not borrow to repay a loan, especially from informal moneylenders.
4. **Talk to the lender.** Banks often agree to restructure when asked early.
5. **Look for help.** In India, banks run credit counselling centres, and RBI-recognised ones exist. A trusted, qualified adviser is better than an agent offering to settle your loan for a fee.
6. **Protect your health.** Don't skip meals or sleep.
7. **Tell one person you trust.** Shame grows in silence.

## Habits of Lakshmi
Traditional teaching ties wealth to a few habits: earn honestly, save a part of every income, pay what you owe, give some away, keep the home clean and treat workers well. They are old ideas, and they match what financial counsellors say.

## What to avoid
- Anyone who promises that a ritual will make you rich or clear all debts for a fee.
- Lotteries and betting as a way out.
- Hiding the problem from family.

## A closer look at what Lakshmi stands for
In the old pictures, Lakshmi sits on a lotus and gold coins fall from her hand. Many people stop there. But her lotus is a quiet reminder too: it grows in mud and stays clean. The tradition has often said that wealth is welcome, but it should be earned in clean ways. The eight forms of Lakshmi include not only money but also courage, learning, victory, food and family. If you would like to know them, read [Ashtalakshmi, the eight forms of Lakshmi](/ashtalakshmi-the-eight-forms-of-lakshmi-and-what-they-mean/).

When money is tight, it can feel as if wealth is the only thing that matters. Taking time to remember the other forms, such as courage and learning, can help you see that you still have resources.

## A simple Friday routine
1. Clean the place where you pray, even a small corner.
2. Light a lamp and sit quietly for a minute.
3. Say the Lakshmi mantra above, slowly, 11 or 108 times, as you can.
4. Sit down afterwards with your notebook and look at one number: what you owe, what you earn, or what you can pay this month.
5. Write one action for the week, such as calling the lender or cancelling an unused subscription.

The prayer calms you, and the notebook moves you. Together they make a real week.

## Questions people ask
**Should I pray to all three?**
You can, but there is no need. Choose the one that feels closest. Many people pray to Lakshmi on Fridays and to Narasimha on Saturdays, as the verses above suggest.

**Is it all right to ask for money in prayer?**
Yes. The tradition has always allowed people to pray for their needs. Pray also for wisdom, because the right decisions matter more than a sudden windfall.

**What about Deepavali?**
Deepavali and Dhanteras are times when many families honour Lakshmi and Kubera, clean the home and settle accounts. If you want the dates, see our [Deepavali 2026 guide](/deepavali-2026-dates-dhanteras-naraka-chaturdashi-lakshmi-puja/).

**What if I owe a family member?**
Be honest with them early and offer a plan, even a small one. A clear conversation is often kinder than silence, and it protects the relationship.

*Prayer brings calm and hope, and many people find it a real strength. It works best alongside practical steps. No mantra guarantees an outcome, and no one should pay large sums to anyone who promises one.*
