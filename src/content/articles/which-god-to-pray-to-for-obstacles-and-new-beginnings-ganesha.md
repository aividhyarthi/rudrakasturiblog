You have a plan. A new job, a business, a house, a wedding. And it keeps stalling. A document is delayed, a person doesn't reply, a date falls through. In every Hindu home, the advice is the same: pray to Ganesha.

## Why him
Ganesha is *Vighnaharta*, the remover of obstacles. That is why every puja, every wedding and every new book begins with his name. You can read the full set of stories in [why Ganesha is worshipped first](/why-ganesha-is-worshipped-first/). The idea is simple: before you start anything, you ask the doorkeeper to let you in.

## The mantras
**1. The short name mantra**
*Om Gam Ganapataye Namah* (ఓం గం గణపతయే నమః)
*Meaning:* I bow to Ganapati. *Gam* is his seed sound.
*How:* 11 or 108 times, before beginning a task.

**2. The prayer before any work**
*Vakratunda Mahakaya Suryakoti Samaprabha,*
*Nirvighnam Kuru Me Deva Sarvakaryeshu Sarvada.*
*Meaning:* O Lord of the curved trunk and mighty body, as bright as a million suns, make all my undertakings free of obstacles, always.
*How:* Say it once before an exam, an interview, a journey or a signing.

**3. The opening verse of every puja**
*Shuklambaradharam Vishnum Shashivarnam Chaturbhujam,*
*Prasannavadanam Dhyayet Sarvavighnopashantaye.*
*Meaning:* I meditate on the one in white, all-pervading, moon-coloured, four-armed, with a gracious face, for the calming of all obstacles.

All of these are on our [Ganesha mantras page](/mantras/ganesha/).

## How to pray
1. **Pick a time.** Wednesday and Chaturthi are his days. Many start any new venture on a Wednesday. See [Sankashti Chaturthi](/sankashti-chaturthi-october-2026-date-moonrise-and-vrat/).
2. **Light a lamp** in front of a picture or small idol.
3. **Offer** durva grass (a bunch of 21), red hibiscus and a sweet, such as a modak or laddu.
4. **Chant** a mantra above.
5. **State your intention in a sentence.** Say what you are about to do and ask for a clear path.
6. **Finish** with a moment of quiet.

## What the practical side looks like
Many obstacles are not mysterious. Here is a short checklist:
- **Is something missing?** A document, a call, an approval.
- **Are you waiting on someone?** Politely follow up.
- **Is there a fear?** Sometimes we delay because we are afraid.
- **Do you have help?** Ask a friend, a mentor or a professional.

The tradition has a phrase for this: *daivam and purushakara*, divine help and human effort. Both are needed. Ganesha is the one who helps you start. The effort is yours.

## A small ritual for a new start
On the day before you begin something, place a coconut before Ganesha, say the Vakratunda verse and then break the coconut at a temple. It is a widely followed custom, and it focuses the mind.
