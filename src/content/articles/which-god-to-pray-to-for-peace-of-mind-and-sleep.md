It is two in the morning. You are tired, and you cannot stop thinking. A worry that seemed small at dinner now seems enormous. People through the ages have used certain prayers for exactly this hour.

## Vishnu in repose
One of the best-known verses of calm is to Vishnu resting on the serpent Adisesha in the ocean of milk.

*Shantakaram Bhujagashayanam Padmanabham Suresham,*
*Vishvadharam Gaganasadrisham Meghavarnam Shubhangam,*
*Lakshmikantam Kamalanayanam Yogibhirdhyanagamyam,*
*Vande Vishnum Bhavabhayaharam Sarvalokaikanatham.*

*Meaning:* I bow to Vishnu: peaceful in form, resting on the serpent, lotus-navelled, lord of the gods, support of the world, vast as the sky, dark as a cloud, beautiful, husband of Lakshmi, lotus-eyed, reached by yogis in meditation, remover of the fear of rebirth, the one lord of all worlds.

Read it slowly. Many people find that the picture itself, a calm figure resting on the sea, settles the mind.

## Dakshinamurthy
Dakshinamurthy is Shiva as the young teacher who teaches in silence, sitting under a banyan tree. The mantra is:

*Om Namah Pranavarthaya Shuddhajnanaikamurtaye,*
*Nirmalaya Prashantaya Dakshinamurtaye Namah.*
*Meaning:* Salutations to Dakshinamurthy, the meaning of Om, the one form of pure knowledge, spotless and peaceful.

Thursday is his day. See the [Dakshinamurthy mantras](/mantras/dakshinamurthy/).

## The Moon
In tradition, Chandra is linked with the mind. Monday evenings, when the Moon rises, are a good time for a quiet prayer. *Om Som Somaya Namah* is the short mantra.

## Om Namah Shivaya
The simple five-syllable mantra works for many. See [Shiva mantras](/mantras/shiva/).

## A bedtime routine
1. **Dim the lights an hour before bed.**
2. **Wash your face and feet.** A cool wash tells the body the day is over.
3. **Light a small lamp** or sit in the dark, if safe.
4. **Say a verse** such as Shantakaram, slowly, once or three times.
5. **Do three long out-breaths.**
6. **Lie down and go through the body,** as in [yoga nidra](/yoga-for-sleep-an-evening-routine-that-helps-you-wind-down/).

## If your mind is racing
- Write the worry down and put it aside for tomorrow.
- Keep the phone away from the bed.
- Avoid heavy food and caffeine late.
- Don't lie in bed for more than twenty minutes awake. Get up and read something quiet.

## The Gita on the mind
Krishna says the mind is restless but can be held by practice and detachment. See [the Gita and overthinking](/gita-on-overthinking-the-restless-mind-and-how-to-hold-it/).

## A closer look at the Shantakaram verse
The first word, *shantakaram*, means one whose form is peace. The picture is of Vishnu lying on the great serpent in the ocean of milk. The sea is calm, the serpent is a bed and the god is awake and at ease, with Lakshmi beside him.

It is worth noticing what the verse does not say. It does not ask for anything. It does not mention your trouble. It only describes calm, and the person saying it borrows a little from the description. That is why many people find it easier than a prayer full of requests, when they are tired and cannot think of the right words.

## A closer look at Dakshinamurthy
Dakshinamurthy sits facing south, under a banyan tree, with a hand raised in a teaching gesture. In the traditional picture, the teacher is young and the students around him are old. They have read everything, and still come away with questions. He teaches in silence, and their doubts melt.

For a mind that will not stop talking, the picture is a gentle suggestion: the answer is not another thought. It is the quiet in which thoughts settle. You can look at an image of Dakshinamurthy, or just imagine the banyan tree, for a minute before bed.

## Making the room ready
A few practical points go well with any prayer.

- **Keep the bedroom cool, dark and quiet** as far as you can.
- **Set a regular time for sleep and waking,** even on holidays.
- **Keep the last hour screen-free.** A bright phone tells the mind it is still daytime.
- **Have a notebook by the bed.** If a thought will not leave, write it down and tell yourself it will be there in the morning.
- **Do not use the prayer as a test.** If you say it and still lie awake, you have not failed. Rest your body anyway.

## Questions people ask
**Can I say the verse silently in bed?**
Yes. Many people say it in the mind, matching it to the slow breath. The sound does not have to be loud.

**Does it matter which god I choose?**
Choose the one you feel close to. If you have a family deity, begin there. The point is to have something steady to hold, and this is why people have used these prayers for centuries.

## When to see a doctor
If you have trouble sleeping for more than three weeks, or if you feel sad or anxious most of the time, please see a doctor. Tele-MANAS (14416) is a free helpline for mental health in India.
