Sleep problems rarely start in the bedroom. They start in the two hours before it, when the body is still running on the speed of the day. If you jump from a laptop to a pillow, no wonder the mind will not slow down.

A short wind-down routine tells the body that the day is over. Here is one you can do in twenty minutes.

## 8:30 pm: dim the lights
Turn down the lights in the house and put the phone away from the bed. Bright light and screens tell the brain it is still day.

## 9:00 pm: the routine
**1. Child's pose (two minutes).** Kneel, rest your forehead on the floor and let the back round. Breathe slowly.

**2. Cat and cow (one minute).** Slow rounds to release the back.

**3. Reclined butterfly (three minutes).** Lie on your back with the soles of your feet together and the knees falling open. Put one hand on your belly. Breathe in for four, out for six.

**4. Gentle supine twist (two minutes each side).** Drop your knees to one side and rest.

**5. Legs up the wall (five minutes).** Rest your legs up a wall with your hips close to it. Skip this if you have glaucoma or it makes you dizzy.

**6. Humming breath (one minute).** Breathe in, hum on the exhale. Five rounds.

## In bed: yoga nidra
Lie on your back and move your attention slowly from the toes to the head, one part at a time: right foot, left foot, ankles, calves, and so on. If your mind wanders, bring it back to the next part. Most people are asleep before they reach the head. There are free recordings in English, Telugu, Tamil and other languages.

## A mantra if you prefer
Repeat quietly *Om Namo Narayanaya* or recite the verse *Shantakaram Bhujagashayanam*, a verse of calm to Vishnu. You can find it on our [Vishnu mantra page](/mantras/vishnu/).

## Habits that matter more than any pose
- **Go to bed and get up at the same time,** even on weekends.
- **No caffeine after the afternoon.**
- **Keep the bedroom dark and cool.**
- **Finish dinner at least two hours before bed.** Heavy food keeps the body busy.
- **Don't lie in bed worrying.** If you can't sleep after twenty minutes, get up, sit somewhere dim and read something calm until you feel sleepy.

## What not to do
- Avoid vigorous exercise in the last two hours.
- Don't use pranayama that wakes the body, such as Kapalabhati, at night.
- Don't rely on sleeping pills or alcohol. Talk to a doctor before taking any.

## When to see a doctor
If you have been sleeping badly for more than three weeks, if you snore loudly and stop breathing, if you feel very sleepy during the day or if your mood has dropped, see a doctor. Sleep apnoea, thyroid problems and depression can all affect sleep, and all are treatable.

## A last thought
Many families in the South still end the day with a lamp, a short prayer and a glass of milk. There is wisdom in that. It is a wind-down routine that every grandmother already knew.

## If the floor is not for you
Not everyone can kneel or lie flat comfortably. You can adapt the routine without losing its purpose:
- **Child's pose:** rest your arms and forehead on a pillow on a low table, or sit on a chair and fold forward over your knees.
- **Reclined butterfly:** put a cushion under each knee so that the hips are not strained.
- **Legs up the wall:** if it is uncomfortable, lie on your back on the bed and simply rest your calves on a pile of pillows.
- **Everything else:** can be done in bed.

If a movement causes pain, stop.

## A shorter version for tired nights
On some nights, twenty minutes is too much. Try this instead, in about ten minutes:
1. Dim the lights and put the phone in another room.
2. Do reclined butterfly for three minutes.
3. Do a supine twist for one minute each side.
4. Do five rounds of humming breath.
5. Lie in bed and do the slow body scan.

A small routine that you repeat is better than a long one you skip. If it becomes a habit, the body begins to feel sleepy as soon as you start.

## Questions people ask
**What if I wake in the night?**
It is common to wake once or twice. Try not to check the time or your phone. Keep the breath slow, repeat your mantra, or do the body scan again.

**I am pregnant. Is it safe?**
Many gentle poses are fine, but some, such as lying flat on the back for long periods, may not suit later pregnancy. Check with your doctor or midwife.

## Small things for the daytime
Good sleep often starts in the morning:
- Get some natural light soon after waking, even for ten minutes.
- Notice what you eat and drink in the evening. Heavy or spicy meals and strong tea may keep some people awake.

If you like to end the day with a prayer, it fits well here. A lamp, a few quiet words and a moment of thanks for the day can clear the mind of what is left undone.
