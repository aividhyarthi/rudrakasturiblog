The Sri Sita Ramachandraswamy Temple is in Bhadrachalam town, on the Godavari river, in Bhadradri Kothagudem district, Telangana. (Some older listings still place it in Khammam district.)

## History
The temple is generally credited to the 17th-century devotee Kancherla Gopanna, known as Bhadrachala Ramadasu. Accounts differ on whether he built it or repaired it. Tradition says Gopanna was imprisoned in Golconda Fort by the last Qutb Shahi ruler, Abul Hasan Tana Shah, over the money he spent on the temple, and that Rama appeared to the ruler in a dream and repaid it. This is tradition, not verified history.

## The deities
The main image shows Rama as Vaikuntha Rama with four arms, with Sita and Lakshmana beside him. Devotees regard the idols as swayambhu, meaning self-manifested.

## Festivals
Sri Rama Navami, on Chaitra Shuddha Navami, is the biggest festival. The Sri Rama Kalyanamahotsavam, the ceremonial wedding of Rama and Sita, is held at the temple as part of a Brahmotsavam. Dates change every year with the lunar calendar.

## Before you visit
Timings, ticket prices, dress rules and booking systems change often, and festival days bring very large crowds. Check the temple's official channels for current details before you travel. Legends on this page are traditional beliefs, reported as such, not verified history.
