The Mallikarjuna Swamy Temple is in Srisailam, Nandyal district, Andhra Pradesh, in the forested Nallamala Hills on the bank of the Krishna river. The deities are Mallikarjuna (Shiva) and Bhramaramba (Parvati).

## Why it is significant
The temple is counted among the 12 Jyotirlingas of Shiva and is also regarded as one of the Shakti Peethas of Parvati. Sources describe it as unusual in being both a Jyotirlinga and a Shakti Peetha at the same site.

## Festivals
The Maha Shivaratri Brahmotsavam runs for about 11 days in the month of Magha, usually February or March. Rituals include vahana sevas for the deities, the Lingodbhava Maharudrabhishekam, the Shiva-Parvati Kalyanotsavam and the Rathotsavam. Many devotees walk to the temple as padayatris through the Nallamala forest.

Dates change every year with the lunar calendar. Check the temple's announcements for the current year.

## Before you visit
Timings, ticket prices, dress rules and booking systems change often, and festival days bring very large crowds. Check the temple's official channels for current details before you travel. Legends on this page are traditional beliefs, reported as such, not verified history.
