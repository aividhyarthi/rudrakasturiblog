The Sri Raja Rajeshwara Swamy Temple is in Vemulawada, in Rajanna Sircilla district, Telangana. The presiding deity is Shiva as Raja Rajeshwara, known locally as Rajanna.

## Why it is called Dakshina Kashi
The complex also holds two Vaishnava shrines, Ananta Padmanabha Swamy and Seetharama Chandra Swamy. Because of this the temple is called Harihara Kshetram, and it is popularly known as Dakshina Kashi.

## History
Vemulawada was the capital of the Vemulawada Chalukyas, who ruled from about 750 to 973 CE. Sources date the temple differently: one says it was built between 760 and 973 CE, another between the 9th and 10th centuries.

## Festivals and customs
The temple draws the largest crowds at Maha Shivaratri and during the Kartika month. Pilgrims customarily bathe in the Dharma Gundam tank before darshan. Legends about the origin of the shrine come from its sthalapurana and are not verified history.

## Before you visit
Timings, ticket prices, dress rules and booking systems change often, and festival days bring very large crowds. Check the temple's official channels for current details before you travel. Legends on this page are traditional beliefs, reported as such, not verified history.
