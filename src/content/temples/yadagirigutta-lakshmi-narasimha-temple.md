The Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy Temple is on a hill at Yadagirigutta, in Yadadri Bhuvanagiri district, Telangana. The hill was renamed Yadadri in 2016, according to a report from that year.

## Why it is significant
Local legend says the deity appeared here in five forms: Jwala Narasimha, Yogananda Narasimha, Ugra Narasimha, Gandaberunda Narasimha and Lakshmi Narasimha. For this reason the site is worshipped as a Panch Narasimha Kshetram. This is a traditional belief.

## History
Guides describe the temple as ancient, with one saying it was built before the 10th century. An inscription at Kolanupaka dated 1148 CE, from the Western Chalukya ruler Tribhuvana Malla, mentions a visit to a temple of the deity.

## Timings
Published timings differ between sources and have changed over the years. We are not listing them here. Please check the temple's official channels.

## Before you visit
Timings, ticket prices, dress rules and booking systems change often, and festival days bring very large crowds. Check the temple's official channels for current details before you travel. Legends on this page are traditional beliefs, reported as such, not verified history.
