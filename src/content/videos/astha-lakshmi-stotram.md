## Meaning in English
Each verse praises Lakshmi in one of her eight forms. The first four lines describe her, and the last line names the form.

1. **Adi Lakshmi** (the original Lakshmi). Worshipped by the good, beautiful, the beloved of Madhava, the sister of the moon, golden. Honoured by sages, she gives liberation and speaks sweetly. The Vedas praise her.
2. **Dhanya Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of grain and food). She destroys the faults of the Kali age, has the form of the Vedas, rose from the ocean of milk, is auspicious and lives in mantras. Gods take refuge at her feet.
3. **Dhairya Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of courage). Victorious and excellent, a Vaishnavi of Bhrigu's line, in the form of mantra. She fulfils prayers quickly and spreads wisdom. She removes the fear of worldly life and frees people from sin.
4. **Gaja Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of royal power, with elephants). She destroys bad fortune and gives all fruits. She is surrounded by chariots, elephants, horses and attendants. Hari, Hara and Brahma worship her, and she removes suffering.
5. **Santana Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of children and continuing family). She charms and spreads love and knowledge. She is an ocean of good qualities, wishes well to the world and is praised in the seven musical notes. Gods, demons and sages bow to her.
6. **Vijaya Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of victory). Seated on the lotus, she grants the good path and spreads wisdom and music. She is honoured by the teacher Shankara.
7. **Vidya Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of learning). Like Bharati (Saraswati), she is adorned with jewels and removes sorrow. She gives the nine treasures, destroys the impurities of the Kali age and grants the fruits people long for.
8. **Dhana Lakshmi** (Lakshmi of wealth). The sound of drums and conches fills her presence. She is worshipped through the Vedas, Puranas and epics and shows the Vedic path.

## How this helps
The eight forms stand for eight kinds of well-being: a good beginning, food, courage, status, family, victory, learning and wealth. Devotees recite the stotram for stability and a good household and for all those forms of prosperity, not just money. It is a prayer, and honest work, saving and generosity have to go with it.

## How and when to chant it
- **Days:** Fridays, Varalakshmi Vratam ([Varalakshmi Vratam](/festivals/varalakshmi-vratam/)), Dhanteras and the Lakshmi Puja of Deepavali ([Deepavali](/festivals/deepavali/)).
- **How:** after a bath, with a lamp lit before Lakshmi's picture, and a few flowers or a lotus if you have one. Recite once, slowly.
- **Learn more:** our piece on [the eight forms of Lakshmi and what they mean](/ashtalakshmi-the-eight-forms-of-lakshmi-and-what-they-mean/) and the guide to [Friday Lakshmi worship](/friday-lakshmi-worship-mantras-and-how-to-do-it/).

## The story behind it
Lakshmi is said to have risen from the ocean of milk when the gods and demons churned it, and she chose Vishnu as her husband. In her eight forms, she is worshipped together in Ashtalakshmi temples, such as the well-known one at Besant Nagar in Chennai. The hymn's verse to Vijaya Lakshmi mentions "Shankara Deshika", a teacher, but we have not found a reliable source for who composed the whole stotram, so we do not name an author.
