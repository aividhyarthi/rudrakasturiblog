## Meaning in English
- **Om Mahalakshmyai cha vidmahe:** Om, we know (we reflect on) the great Lakshmi.
- **Vishnupatnyai cha dhimahi:** and we meditate on her, the wife of Vishnu.
- **Tanno Lakshmih prachodayat:** may that Lakshmi inspire and guide us.

The mantra follows the pattern of the Gayatri mantra ("vidmahe, dhimahi, prachodayat"): know, meditate, and ask to be guided. Notice that it asks for guidance and not for wealth. The prosperity of Lakshmi is understood to include steadiness, well-being and good fortune.

## How this helps
Devotees chant the Lakshmi Gayatri for prosperity, stability in money matters, peace in the home and a clear head. It is a prayer for good fortune, and it goes best with honest work, fair dealing and generosity. No mantra replaces a plan for your finances.

## How and when to chant it
- **Days:** Fridays are Lakshmi's day. Varalakshmi Vratam ([Varalakshmi Vratam](/festivals/varalakshmi-vratam/)) and the Lakshmi Puja of Deepavali ([Deepavali](/festivals/deepavali/)) are the great occasions.
- **Time:** early morning or at dusk, with a lamp lit.
- **How many times:** 11, 21 or 108 times is common. A mala of 108 beads is used, and a lotus-seed mala is the traditional choice for Lakshmi.
- **Listening:** the video above runs for about six minutes. Play it and chant along.

## The story behind it
Lakshmi is said to have risen from the ocean of milk when the gods and asuras churned it, holding a lotus. She chose Vishnu as her husband, and since then she is called Vishnupatni, the wife of Vishnu, as the mantra says. Read more on our [Lakshmi page](/gods/lakshmi/).
