## Meaning in English
Each verse praises the lingam in a different way and then repeats the same line.

1. The lingam worshipped by Brahma, Vishnu and the gods; pure and shining; it destroys the sorrows of birth and death.
2. Worshipped by gods and the great sages; it burned Kama, the god of desire; compassionate; it ended the pride of Ravana.
3. Smeared with all fragrant pastes; it grows wisdom; honoured by siddhas, gods and asuras.
4. Adorned with gold and great jewels; wrapped by the king of serpents; it destroyed the sacrifice of Daksha.
5. Anointed with saffron and sandal; decorated with lotus garlands; it destroys the sins a person has gathered.
6. Worshipped with devotion by hosts of gods; shining like a crore of suns.
7. Set on an eight-petalled lotus; the cause of all creation; it removes the eight kinds of poverty.
8. Worshipped by the guru of the gods and the best of the gods with flowers; the supreme beyond the supreme, the Self of all.

The closing verse says that whoever recites this hymn near Shiva reaches the world of Shiva and rejoices with him.

## How this helps
Devotees recite the Lingashtakam to calm the mind, to ask forgiveness for past mistakes and to feel close to Shiva during worship. The hymn itself speaks of the destruction of sin and sorrow and of poverty. These are promises of faith, not guarantees, and they go together with honest effort in daily life.

## How and when to chant it
- **Days:** Mondays, Pradosham (the thirteenth tithi twice a month, see [Pradosham dates](/pradosham/)), Maha Shivaratri ([Maha Shivaratri](/festivals/maha-shivaratri/)) and the Mondays of Karthika ([Karthika Somavaram](/karthika-somavaram-the-monday-fast-and-the-worship-of-shiva/)).
- **Where:** at home before a lingam or Shiva picture, or at a temple while water, milk or bilva leaves are offered.
- **How:** after a bath, with a lamp lit. Many people recite it once and then spend a few quiet minutes with the mantra Om Namah Shivaya.

## The stories behind the verses
- **Kama burned (verse 2).** When the god of desire shot an arrow at Shiva in meditation, Shiva opened his third eye and burned him to ash.
- **Ravana's pride (verse 2).** Ravana tried to lift Mount Kailasa, Shiva's home, and Shiva pressed it down with his toe. Tradition says Ravana then sang the Shiva Tandava Stotram to him. Read the full story in [Ravana and Shiva: the Tandava Stotram and the lifted mountain](/ravana-and-shiva-the-tandava-stotram-and-the-lifted-mountain/).
- **Daksha's sacrifice (verse 4).** After the death of Sati at her father Daksha's sacrifice, Shiva's anger took form and destroyed that sacrifice.
