## Meaning in English
"Govinda" is a name of Krishna and Vishnu, usually explained as "the protector of cows" or "the one who is found by the Vedas". Calling Govinda is calling on the Lord. The names are grouped, and many of them point to a story:

- **Names of the Lord of Tirumala.** Srinivasa, Venkatesha, Venkataramana, Seshadri-nilaya (he who lives on Seshadri), Tirumalavasa (he who lives at Tirumala), Padmavati-priya (beloved of Padmavati).
- **Names of Krishna's childhood.** Gokula Nandana, Nandanandana (Nanda's son), Navanitachora (the butter-thief), Pashupalaka (cowherd), Venuganapriya (lover of the flute), Govardhanoddhara (who lifted Mount Govardhana).
- **Names of the ten avatars.** Matsya, Kurma, Varaha, Narasimha, Vamana, Bhrigurama (Parashurama), Dasharatha-nandana (Rama), Balaramanuja (Krishna, younger brother of Balarama), Bauddha and Kalki. See our guide to [the ten avatars of Vishnu](/dashavatara-the-ten-avatars-of-vishnu-explained/).
- **Names that promise protection.** Papavimochana (remover of sin), Kashtanivarana (remover of hardship), Apadbandhava (friend in distress), Anatharakshaka (protector of the helpless), Gajarajarakshaka and Ibharajarakshaka (protector of the king of elephants, Gajendra).
- **Names of the Lord's form.** Neelameghashyama (dark as a rain cloud), Pundarikaksha (lotus-eyed), Shankhachakradhara (bearer of conch and discus), Garudavahana (who rides Garuda), Sheshashayana (who rests on Shesha).

The song ends with the lines "Sri Srinivasa Govinda, Sri Venkatesha Govinda" and a prayer for peace: "Om shanti shanti shantih".

## How this helps
Devotees sing the Govinda Namalu for blessings, protection on a journey and peace of mind, and to prepare for a visit to Tirumala. Because every line is simple and repeated, it is one of the easiest hymns to learn and to sing with a family or a group. The words are a plea and not a promise: they are a way to remember the Lord throughout the day.

## How and when to chant it
- **Days:** Saturdays are Venkateswara's day (see our guide to [Saturday worship of Venkateswara](/saturday-venkateswara-shani-hanuman-how-to-worship/)), and Ekadashi days, the Brahmotsavam and the Vaikuntha Ekadashi are the great occasions.
- **How:** after a bath, with a lamp lit, sing the names with the refrain. Many families sing it before the evening lamp, and pilgrims call "Govinda, Govinda" as they climb to Tirumala.
- **Listening:** the video above is about six minutes long, so you can sing along.

## The story behind it
The Lord of Tirumala is called Venkateswara because he stands on the Venkata hill, one of the seven hills of Tirumala. He is also worshipped as Srinivasa and Balaji. The name Padmavati-priya recalls his marriage to the princess Padmavati, and the names of the avatars remind us that the Lord of the hills is Vishnu himself.
