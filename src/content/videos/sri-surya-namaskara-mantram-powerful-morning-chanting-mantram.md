## Meaning in English
The twelve names are all names of the Sun, each pointing to a quality:

1. **Mitra**: the friend.
2. **Ravi**: the shining one.
3. **Surya**: the one who sets the world in motion.
4. **Bhanu**: the radiant one.
5. **Khaga**: the one who moves through the sky.
6. **Pusha**: the nourisher.
7. **Hiranyagarbha**: the golden source from which life comes.
8. **Marichi**: the lord of the rays.
9. **Aditya**: the son of Aditi, and also the Sun in each of the twelve months.
10. **Savitri**: the one who gives birth to light and life. The Gayatri mantra is addressed to him.
11. **Arka**: the one who is worthy of worship, the radiant one.
12. **Bhaskara**: the maker of light.

Each mantra is "Om, (seed sound), (name) in the dative case, namah": "I bow to the Sun in this form."

## How this helps
The mantras are chanted along with the twelve steps of the Surya Namaskar exercise, so that the movement becomes a prayer. Devotees say it brings energy, discipline and gratitude at the start of the day. The physical exercise has real benefits for flexibility and strength, but check with a doctor first if you have back, heart, blood pressure or other health problems, or are pregnant. The mantras are a prayer, not a treatment.

## How and when to use them
- **Time:** sunrise, facing east, on an empty stomach if you do the exercise.
- **How:** stand with palms joined, say the first mantra, and move through the twelve steps, one mantra for each. You can also simply stand and say the twelve in order.
- **Days:** every day, with extra devotion on Sundays and on Ratha Saptami ([Ratha Saptami](/festivals/ratha-saptami/)).

## The story behind it
The Sun is the one god we can see, and worship of the Sun is among the oldest in India. The Vedas address him as Surya and Savitri, and the Ramayana has the sage Agastya teaching Rama the Aditya Hrudayam before the battle with Ravana.
